Privacy

What we keep,
and where.

Second Growth is a free game made by one person, Cole Weiler. There is no account and no ads. This page says what is collected, who holds it, and how to ask for it to be removed. Updated 13 November 2026.

Details

Your game

Your farm, settings and progress are saved in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us. Clearing the site’s data in your browser deletes them.

Anonymous play data

The game sends named events (such as which tool was used, the year reached, a species discovered, or a frame-rate sample) to PostHog in its US cloud. It uses a random identifier kept in the browser’s local storage, no cookies, no session recordings and no tracking of clicks or what you type. Like any web request, it reaches PostHog with your browser type and your network address, which PostHog uses to work out a rough country. Crashes are reported with the error message and file name. We use it to see where the game breaks or confuses people.

To stop it, turn off Share anonymous play data in the game’s Settings, under Privacy. Browsers set to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are respected automatically. Copies of the game run from a local address send nothing.

Feedback

When you press Send in the feedback box, we receive your rating, your message, the contact you chose to add (optional), the landscape you were on, a snapshot of the game state (such as the year and species counts) and the random player identifier. The message is emailed to Cole through Resend and a copy goes to PostHog as an event. Only the rating, landscape, time and player identifier are kept in a small database. The server that handles it logs no messages, contacts or email addresses. Please don’t put anything private in a message.

The monthly email

If you subscribe, your email address is held by Kit, which sends the email. Every email has an unsubscribe link. The signup is for players 13 and older; younger players should ask a parent first. Subscribing is never needed to play.

This website

The website (not the game) loads Google Tag Manager for visit statistics, unless your browser says Do Not Track. Fonts come from Google Fonts. The signup, feedback and rating forms on the website send to the same server as the game’s.

On itch.io

If you play on itch.io, itch.io’s own privacy policy applies to what it collects, on top of the above.

Children

Playing needs no personal information, and the game collects none on purpose. We don’t knowingly ask for personal information from anyone under 13: if a younger player’s parent tells us an email address or message was sent, we will delete it.

Questions and deletion

Write to info@secondgrowth.earth to ask what we hold about you, to have it deleted, or to be taken off the monthly email. Send the address you subscribed or wrote from. Anonymous play data can’t be tied to a person, so we can only delete it if you send the player identifier from your browser (key sg-player in local storage). We’ll answer within a few weeks. Changes to this page are dated above.
