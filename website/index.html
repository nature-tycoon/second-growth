Before you put down roots

A few field notes.

How do I play?

Open the game in your browser and choose a map. The in-game introduction will help you get started with planting, restoring habitat, and exploring your landscape.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Second Growth runs directly in a modern browser with WebGL support. A larger screen gives you more room to explore; touch controls are available too.

Will my progress be saved?

The game autosaves in your browser on this device. Use the same browser and website to return to your land. Private browsing and clearing site data can remove saves.

Can I buy animals?

Wildlife follows the habitat. Restore food, water, shelter, and connections to the surrounding landscape, and animals will find their way in when conditions suit them.
