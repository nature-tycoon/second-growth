Turn your phone sideways

Second Growth plays in landscape. Rotate your phone to keep restoring the land.

Screen won't turn? Switch off rotation lock in Control Center (iPhone) or Quick Settings (Android).

Play from your Home Screen

Add Second Growth for a full-screen game without the browser bars.

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Share (the square with an upward arrow). It may be inside the page menu.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen. If you see Open as Web App, turn it on, then tap Add.
  3. Open Second Growth from its new Home Screen icon.
  1. Open this page in Chrome and tap the three-dot menu beside the address bar.
  2. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app. Some versions put Install inside Install and create shortcut. Follow the prompts.
  3. Open Second Growth from its new Home Screen icon.
