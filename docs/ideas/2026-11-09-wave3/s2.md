# Wave3 session 2: fruit birds and seabirds eat

Drawing and motion only: no population, plant, hunger or seed numbers change (the meals feed nothing, the seabirds never take a drawn reef fish), so no balance run.

## Done
- **Fruit birds eat in fruiting crowns.** New trait `eatsFruit` (kind defaults: toucans and hornbills `'toss'`, macaws and parrots `'chew'`; the Sumatran bulbul `'peck'` on its species). In season a fruit bird flies to a fruiting tree within 9 tiles (`fruitAt`, the same mature in-season fruit the primates use), takes a 2.5–4.5-day meal there (a few seconds at watch pace), then is full for 2–6 days and goes off to preen, bathe or perch. The meal ends at once if the crown burns or stops fruiting. No second seed rule: dispersal stays with `seedTravel` (0027).
- **Drawn fruit meals** (`eating-pose.js fruitHead`): toucans and hornbills reach, pluck and toss the fruit back; parrots bite with their head low and look up now and then; bulbuls peck and gulp. Checked in the Amazon: a toucan sits on a fig crown with its head going reach (0.5), toss (-0.6), level, reach. Status reads "Eating fruit in a treetop"; watch mode rates it like woodpeckers.
- **Boobies plunge-dive** (new fishing style `dive`, booby kind default `fishes: 'dive'`): fly out high (alt 1.6), glide round over the spot looking down, then plunge bill-first with wings swept back. The drop speeds up as it falls (drawn 4.4 → 1.8, the sea surface). It stays under a moment and either surfaces with a fish to swallow afloat, or sits a beat and climbs back up. A splash goes up where it enters, a ring where it surfaces. Strike odds 0.3, 4 tries a bout.
- **Noddies dip** (style `dip`, `fishes: 'dip'` on the noddy): fly low (alt 0.45), swoop to snatch at the surface on the wing (a small ring), rise and look again. A catch is swallowed on the wing. Odds 0.22, 6 tries.
- Seabirds fish the open sea (`seaTile`: off the reef's `dryLand`) and come up only with fish marked `surfaceFish` (longtom, chromis), never a drawn agent. In the browser on the reef, three boobies made about eight dives in 16 s and came up with 3 fish.
- `fishingProfileFor` now asks the `fishes` trait first. The old key table `BY_KEY` is still there for the river fishers; see the ideas below.
- Checks: `fishing-check` gains booby and noddy cases (most strikes miss, booby goes under from high up, noddy never does, the catch is a surface fish). `bird-behavior-check` gains a fruit-bird case on Amazon and Sumatra (no fruit out of season, flies to the fruiting crown, short meal in the crown, moves on, leaves if it burns). `motion-sweep-check` all within limits before and after. `perf-guard-check` pass. `species-name-count` ok (no new name checks). `data-contracts`, `flight-canopy`, `moments`, `animal-rhythm` and `woodpecker-trunk` checks pass. No `play-profile`: nothing new is drawn but a handful of splash particles.
- Rules lines added to 0030 (seabird styles, fruit meals) and 0039 (`fruitHead`).

## New ideas noticed
- The **motion sweep doesn't cover fliers or perched birds** (it says so in its header), so dives, swoops and crown meals are checked only by `fishing-check`, `bird-behavior-check` and `flight-canopy-check`. A small flier sweep (height jumps, pitch snaps) would catch flight glitches.
- **Kingfishers could go under too.** Their plunge is now the only dive with no splash and no moment under water. The `under` phase and splash would carry over with a line in `seaFishing`-style code.
- **Finish moving fishing to the trait:** give herons, eagles, otters and bears `fishes:` on their kinds or species and delete `BY_KEY` in `data/animal-fishing.js` (it drops a few species-name checks off the ratchet).
- **More fruit birds:** the Chinandega magpie-jay and the PNW waxwings or robins in fruiting shrubs. `fruitAt` looks only at tree crowns today; berry shrubs (piper, psychotria) would need a shrub version.
- **Booby "rafts":** boobies and noddies following a feeding frenzy of trevally or tuna pushing fish to the surface is a classic reef sight (a moment for watch mode).
