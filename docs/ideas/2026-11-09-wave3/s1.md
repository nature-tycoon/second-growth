# Wave3 s1: prey jink sideways

## Done
- Prey that escape by running cut hard sideways as a chasing hunter springs at them or comes within about a body length (decision 0026, "Prey jink sideways"). About an 80° cut, held for roughly a quarter of a second at normal speed (0.4 s at watch pace), up to 3 times a flight with a short breather between, and it doesn't always come off (70%). While the prey is cutting, the hunter loses its quick close-range turn and its snap onto the prey, so it runs on past and has to swing round.
- Chosen by a new trait, `jinks`: on for rabbits, gazelles, impala and deer by body kind. Elk and sambar opt out (big deer run straight). No species-name checks added (`species-name-count` passes).
- Kills kept: the jink cost hunters of jinking prey about 7% of those kills (98 → 91, 6 seeds, game-like step), so the catch roll on jinking prey went from 0.45 to 0.48 (`catchOdds`): 96 after. At the balance step: 264 before, 279 after. The 12-year `predation-soak` and seeded `calibrate` moved both ways within the seed spread (details in 0026).
- `hunt-check` runs seeds 1987, 91 and 5 and judges each limit by the median over the seeds that have enough hunts to judge it (at least half of them; a limit only one seed can judge is printed as a note). Passes.
- Checks run: hunt-check (3 seeds), predation-soak 12, animal-steering-check, motion-sweep-check, species-name-count, data-contracts, animal-life, prey-avoidance, challenging-ecology, consistency, pounce-probe, calibrate all 15 3 (+ PNW and Atlanta on 6 seeds).

## New ideas noticed
- After a missed grab, the hunter keeps running right on top of the prey for about a third of a day (the 0.3-day "no second grab" window, distances of 0.02–0.1 tiles in traces). A small step back or a slower follow during that window would look less like the two bodies overlapping.
- `predation-soak` on 3 seeds swings kills per hunter-year by ±40% for some hunters (coyote, leopard) from any change. Running it on 6 seeds by default would make before/after comparisons more trustworthy.
