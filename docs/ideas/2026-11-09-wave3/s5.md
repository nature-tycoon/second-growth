# Session 5: natural tracker lens

Branch `claude/wave3-s5-tracker-lens-aaed29`.

## Done

- The lens no longer cuts a box. What covers the animal's body opens as an ellipsoid fitted inside its model's box, which shows round on screen and is tighter than the box.
- The canopy gap is sized to the animal. It starts from the animal's outline on screen and flares toward the camera, instead of a fixed 0.85–1.45 tiles. A vole or a beetle gets a small peephole, the crown round a perched bird keeps most of its leaves, and grass and ferns round small animals' feet stay. An elephant still gets a wide gap.
- The look Cole preferred stays: a ragged edge following leaf clumps, and shaded walls.
- Tried and dropped, after Cole's feedback mid-session:
  - A dithered soft edge: "old school".
  - Alpha-to-coverage: the Mac's graphics driver draws it as the same stipple, plus a light ring.
  - A smooth screen-space ellipse: Cole preferred the earlier look.
- New check `tools/tracker-lens-browser-check.mjs [dir] --shots [--gpu]`. It covers vole, frog, wren and elk on PNW, and weaver, dung beetle and elephant on Serengeti, from above, close up and at eye level. With the lens on, the animal shows as much as before (within 1%). From above, a small animal's lens now opens a small fraction of the plants the old lens cleared.
- Before/after pictures are in the shared notes folder (`w3s5-lens-*-before.png` / `-after.png`).
- Checks:
  - `follow-support-browser-check`: passes, palm fronds 90–94% kept (old code 87–93%).
  - `perf-guard-check`: passes.
  - `canopy-reveal-browser-check`: passes fig and meranti, and fails oilpalm by 13.6%. It fails identically on the untouched code, so the failure was already there.
- Owed: `play-profile` timing. Other sessions were running, so I didn't measure it. The shader does the same or less work than before (the box slab test became one ray–ellipsoid test), and draw calls are unchanged.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **The lens looks the same as before on big animals. Is that right?** Recommended: yes. The elk and elephant still get a wide canopy gap, because the gap scales with the animal. If it should be tighter for big animals too, lower the 0.8 in `gap = reach * .8 + .04` in `js/render3d/scene.js`.
2. **Should someone fix the oilpalm case in `canopy-reveal-browser-check`?** It was already failing before this change: palm leaves hide 13.6% of a langur in one view. Recommended: yes, as a small follow-up. It also stops the check before it reaches its Fast-graphics cases.
3. **Do you want a true see-through fade (plants going transparent at the edge)?** It means drawing the plants twice while an animal is tracked. Recommended: no. It costs too much on the low-end target (0004), and the ragged gap reads naturally.

## New ideas noticed

- An animal with a long tail or antlers (langur, elk) gets a gap sized to its tail or antlers. A body-only box (`revealBounds` without tail and antlers) would let the gap hug the body.
- The eye-level lens could ease open only when cover actually blocks the animal, instead of whenever it's selected. Today the code runs either way, but nothing is cut when nothing blocks.
