It breaks decision 0004's "native resolution" wording, so those results would be labelled. Recommend: yes, for Fast only, with a setting to turn it off.

## New ideas noticed
- Send `load_ms` with `game_start` (page start to first drawn frame). The browser's largest-paint time jumped from about 3–4 s to 10–13 s at p90 on Oct 18, the day after the new loading screen, and nothing else measures loading.
- Add a `perf_sample` at 10 s active, so the opening seconds can be told apart from the 30 s sample.
- Drop "ResizeObserver loop completed" in `reportError` (`js/analytics.js`); 44 of the 75 players with errors sent it, and it hides the real ones.
- secondgrowth.earth/play is now where most players come from (139 of 191 game visitors in 30 days); `docs/posthog-player-perf.md` still only names GitHub Pages and itch.io. Worth one line there.
- Only 5 of 49 phone players and 19 of 98 computer players came back on a second day. A PostHog retention view split by map and device would show whether a slow first session predicts not coming back.
