## New ideas noticed
- Phones' frames are ~40 ms with only ~13 ms on the processor. Profile one real iPhone with Safari's timeline before choosing a phone fix.
- Drop "ResizeObserver loop completed" in `reportError`; 44 of the 75 players with errors sent it.
- "Unsuitable ground" refusals are common (median 34 for 30-minute players): show the reason and a suitable mix at the cursor.
- A first-minute payoff (sprouts or a visitor animal on the first stroke) may matter more than any tutorial text; the first goals land only after a game year.
- Chinandega and Atlanta keep first-time players best (8/12, 6/9 to 10 minutes) and Sumatra worst (3/16). If that holds with more data, the map list order matters.
