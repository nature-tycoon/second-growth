## Done

- **What was actually missing.** In the simulation, every creek map's starting creek already reached the
  farm's north row (Amazon, Serengeti, Atlanta, Chinandega, Sumatra). PNW's ditch started one row in, at
  a spring. What made it look as if the creek came from nowhere was the drawn land beyond the fence (14
  tiles of forest, hills, volcano): the creek stopped dead at the fence.
- **The creek now runs on out of sight** on all six creek maps, along the same winding line the map
  already used for it, out to the far edge of the drawn land. It comes in from the north on every map,
  because the north is uphill on every map (foothills, ridge, volcano, hills, the park). Its bed falls
  toward the farm all the way, so the water runs downhill into the farm (0044). Where it's steep (PNW's
  foothills, Atlanta's ridge, Sumatra's hills), it draws as white cascade water. On PNW it stays dry
  while the water model has the ditch dry at the fence.
- **Drawing only.** Only the land beyond the fence is reshaped. The farm's ground, the simulation and
  balance don't move, apart from PNW's one extra ditch tile (next point). The reshaping can run again on
  every load and changes nothing the second time; a save and reload gives identical heights.
- **PNW:** the ditch now begins at the fence (row 0) instead of a row in. A water-gap fence hangs across
  it, so the north fence stays whole: deer still wait at the fence for the opening (0053). Beyond the
  fence it swings north-west at once, so the intro film's stage stays dry ground: no buck or cougar
  mark, no shot's animal, stands in it. The salmonberry opening the buck browses is unchanged.
- **Reef skipped:** no starting creek.
- **Old saves** load unchanged. An old PNW farm keeps its spring a row in, so its creek isn't carried on
  past the fence. The other maps' old saves get the outside creek, since their creek already reached the
  fence.
- **Waiting deer** stand on a bank, not in the creek beyond the fence (`offCreek`). No random draw was
  added, so the simulation's random stream is the same.
- **Checks** (headless, `nice -n 15`): `water-shape-check` gains tests per map: the creek beyond the
  fence at full width, out to the edge, falling toward the farm, carved again unchanged; and deer
  waiting at the fence beside it stand on a bank.
  `waterfall-check`'s "no white water off PNW" now looks at the farm only. Passing: water-shape,
