## Open questions for Cole

1. **PNW's creek drops steeply right beyond the fence.** The forest stands about 3 levels (a few
   metres) above the farm there, so the creek comes down a broad, steep white run in the fence shot.
   *Recommended:* keep it (a Cascade creek off the foothills does that). If it bothers you, make that
   one stretch a small rocky waterfall, like the farm's own falls.
2. **An old PNW farm doesn't get the creek beyond the fence** (its ditch still begins a row in).
   *Recommended:* leave it. Changing an old farm's ground would break "old saves load unchanged", and
   a new farm shows it.
3. **On the other five maps the creek has always been a gap in the north fence** that arriving
   animals can walk through. PNW's creek crossing is closed to arrivals while its fence stands
   (`fencedCreek`), with no fence on the water.
   *Recommended:* leave the other maps as they are. It's been that way all along, and none of them
   opens with the fence step. Say if you want their creeks fenced the same way.
