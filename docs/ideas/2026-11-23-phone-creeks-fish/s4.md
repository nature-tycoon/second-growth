# Session 4: Fish clumping at falls and marsh

## Done

- **Why it came back.** Earlier fixes let fish hop a short dry lip between creek pools (about a third of a tile). Then the water-routes work (2026-11-19) only let a fish's route cross stretches that were deep enough. The game judges depth on each creek tile by a flat sheet of water at its lowest corner. On a steep creek, dropping more than about 0.4 levels a tile, the upper half of every tile reads as dry ground. That is longer than the hop, so the route was refused both ways, at every tile.
  - The waterfall (2026-11-22) is only built in a steep reach, and the steep bed carries on below its plunge pool. So the pool was sealed off: fish in it never left, and fish below never got in.
  - The drawn water there is continuous, because the drawing slopes the sheet down each tile from the water above. The game and the drawing disagreed.
  - Redug creeks make this worse: a dug creek beside a steep reach is shallow in the same way.
- **Second cause, in how fish pick where to go.** A fish won't go back to its last four places. In a small stretch where every pool it can reach is one of those four, it stayed put for good, with nothing ever clearing the list.
- **Fix:**
  - **Fish swim through riffles.** Moving fish now read the drawn, sloping water (`runDepth` in `js/sim/swim-routes.js`). This covers routes, each swimming step, and the steering that puts a fish back in water. On a creek tile each corner takes the water level of the creek tiles round it, as the drawing does. Limits:
    - no more than 0.7 levels over the tile's own water, so a near-fall chute stays a wall
    - never across a beaver dam's line
    - a waterfall keeps its own flat sheet, so no fish climbs it (the 0050 rule holds; `waterfall-check` passes)
  - **Resting and drawing.** Fish still rest and aim for pools. One that stops in a riffle swims on to a pool at its next choice instead of jumping to it. The catfish keeps the old put-back. A fish crossing a riffle is drawn in the running water, not on the bed.
  - **Holding fish.** A fish holding where it is now and then forgets its oldest recent place.
  - **Flood cutoffs unchanged.** Flood cutoffs still judge by the old flat sheet, so which bends get cut is unchanged.
- **Measured** with the new check `node tools/fish-falls-marsh-check.mjs` (headless, 3 s), before → after:

  | | Before | After |
  |---|---|---|
  | Fish time within 3.5 tiles of the fall, fresh PNW | 33% | 1% |
  | Pool fish that went down the reach, fresh PNW | 0 of 4 | 4 of 4 |
  | Pool fish that went down the reach, creeks dug beside the pool and the reach below | – | 4 of 4 |
  | Fish from below that came up to the pool, same dug map | – | 8 of 8 |

  - At a shallow dug marsh, fish spend the same time at its edge as with no marsh there (16.2% vs 16.3%), and none are held by shallows (2% before). They turn away rather than queue.
  - At a dug pond, 43% of fish time is spent inside it.
