# Session 8: Sumatra snakeheads piling up in peat marsh

## Done

- **Snakeheads no longer pile up in peat marsh.** There were three causes. None of them went through the code the 2026-11-20 fix covered.
  - **Escape target past dry ground.** A fish fleeing a monitor lizard darted into a marsh tile that had only a sliver of water along its near edge. The aim point in that tile was too shallow, so it was nudged to the nearest deep water. That water lay in the next tile, past a dry strip the fish couldn't swim. The fish pressed at the edge for days, and the others fleeing the same way piled up behind it.
    - Now the aim point stays in that tile, at the point the fish's route reached it by (`swimPoint`, `js/sim/swim-routes.js`, used in `walkStep`).
    - Leaving fish and points beyond the farm's edge are unchanged.
  - **Long trips ignored dry ground.** A fish setting off for another patch of water (`roam`, and `disperse` for young fish) searched its route with no swim test. Every other fish route already had one. Fish headed across a marsh's dry lip, got stuck, and set off the same way again.
    - Both searches now keep to water the fish can swim (`js/sim/wildlife-intent.js`).
  - **Fleeing fish bunched.** Every fish fleeing an otter took the one tile farthest from it, a dead-end corner.
    - Now a river fish's escape counts against tiles where others of its kind already are or are heading, so they scatter (`evadeHunt`).
    - Only river fish are affected. Herds and other animals flee as before.
- **Why the 2026-11-20 fix stopped holding.** It only made a fish rest where it was when its resting spot was slightly too shallow and close by (within 0.6 tiles). Escapes and long trips never went through that code. Later sessions shifted the shared random stream, which put a monitor or an otter beside these marshes on the default seeds.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Fish stranded in a puddle.** On Sumatra seed 37, nine snakeheads are stranded in a one-tile puddle that the dry season cut off from every other water. That still counts as piling (50 per 1000). One tile can't hold nine fish without crowding.
   - Real snakeheads breathe air and can wriggle short distances over wet ground to the next pool.
   - *Recommend:* let a river fish with the air-breathing trait cross a tile or two of wet marsh ground to the nearest swimmable water when it's stranded. Other stranded fish would die off as the puddle dries. A small session.
2. **The elephant fix needs a close-up look.** The sweep measures the feet at the same two points the fix lifts, so it was bound to pass. Legs splayed across a side-slope could still dip into the ground.
   - *Recommend:* look at an elephant at the foot of a bank in `tools/animal-closeup-review.html`, or in the game zoomed in, before the next film shoot.
3. **A small daily cost.** The first fish to plan a long trip each day now works out the day's swim routes for the whole map: about 7 ms on Amazon and 1–4 ms on Sumatra, then about 0.3 ms per trip.
   - *Recommend:* leave it. It's one short hitch a day, well inside the frame budget. If `play-profile` ever shows it, spread the work over several frames.

## New ideas noticed

- A fish stuck in its target tile for days was never noticed by the stuck-movement recovery, because it kept sliding left and right along the edge. A per-fish "no progress in a day, give up the target" rule would catch any future cause of this.
- The motion sweep's `sunk` could sample the drawn hoof positions, not just two points along the heading, so it can't be satisfied by the very fix it checks.
- `evadeHunt` for herds also sends every animal to the same tile farthest from the hunter. Herds bunching when they flee is real, but the same scatter could suit solitary prey such as duikers or tapirs (a trait, not a species list).
