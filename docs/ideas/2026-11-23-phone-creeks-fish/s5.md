# Session 5: Jaguar rush vs motion sweep

## Done

- **It was a real glitch, not the check misreading it.** On the default seeds an Amazon jaguar crept up on an agouti at walking pace. In a single frame it then started its rush and went straight into a pounce. That happened because it was already inside pounce range (1.4 tiles). The pounce always ran at 3.6 times walking pace, so the cat went from a walk to a flying leap with no burst between them. Nothing teleported (the drawn cat tracked the simulation exactly), but the speed jumped 3.6 times in one frame. Pounces at the end of a real sprint were fine. Real big cats burst a few bounds out of the stalk and then pounce.
- **Fix** (`huntStep` / `startLeap` in `js/sim/animal-life.js`, a new rule line in decision 0026):
  - A pounce now comes only out of a burst. A cat (or a fox or coyote on small prey) leaps once its rush reaches at least half its leap speed. From a creep that's about a tenth of a second on screen, a bound or two.
  - A cat that starts very close (under 0.9 tiles) still pounces, but never more than twice the pace it springs from. Its arc is lower to match, so a slow leap doesn't hang in the air.
  - The cheetah's pounce already starts inside 0.9, so only that speed cap applies to it.
- **Measured** before → after:
  - Motion sweep, default seeds: **fail → pass**. Jaguar position jumps went from 7.0 per 1000 frames to 0.
  - Motion sweep over ten seed pairs (`herd-crawl-probe`): Amazon has 3 failing pairs out of 10, down from 4, because the jaguar pair is gone. No new failing pairs on any map.
  - `hunt-check` and `pounce-probe` output: **identical** to before. Kills, catch rates and leaps are all unchanged.
  - Pounce probe at the coarse 0.1-day step that long runs use: leaps and kills the same over 3 seeds × 150 days (one bobcat leap fewer).
  - `predation-soak` and seeded `calibrate` moved by up to ±35% for a few hunters. That's chaos from the shifted random stream, not a trend: the hunt and pounce probes, which isolate hunting, didn't move.
- **New check:** `tools/pounce-gate-check.mjs` (headless, a few seconds). A slow rush at 1.2 tiles runs on instead of leaping. A fast one leaps. A close, slow one pounces at no more than twice its pace, with a lower arc.

## New ideas noticed

- During a leap, `leapStep` still reports the animal's pace (`a.run`) as the rush pace, not the leap's own speed. So the legs' stride rate doesn't speed up for the spring. It's drawing only and minor, but it would make the jump read as more explosive.
- The motion sweep's allowance is 2.5 times the reported pace. Any future move that's faster than the animal says it is will show up as a "jump", so new bursts (for example a croc's lunge) should report their real pace.
