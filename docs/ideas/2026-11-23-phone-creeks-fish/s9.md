## Done

- **The creek beyond the north fence is now drawn the farm creek's width, along a smooth curve, on every creek map.** The course is generated, not placed by hand: it looked right on PNW, so no hand-placed points were needed.
  - A smooth centre line (`northCreekLine`, `js/maps/water-shape.js`) follows the map's own creek course and joins the farm creek exactly where it crosses the fence. The carve and the water both follow it.
  - The bed is cut to match the line: flat in the middle, rising to the water's edge just past the drawn edge, then the gorge walls. It only ever lowers ground, and only where the old carve already did, so loading a save carves nothing new.
  - The water lies on that bed (bed + water depth at every point), not as one flat sheet per tile. On PNW's steep hillside the flat sheets stood in the air over the next tile's bank, or sank under it. That was the clipping at the top.
  - The water ends at a smooth ribbon the farm creek's width (the same idea as session 6's farm ribbon). PNW's is a little narrower (`northCreekHalf: 0.72` in `js/maps/pnw.js`), because its farm ditch draws narrower than the other maps' creeks.
  - **The pale wedge over the fence** west of PNW's creek mouth came from the off-map water spilling over the farm's bare fence corners. The ribbon now ends it there. A two-tile creek's or a pond's own fence corners keep the farm's edge.
