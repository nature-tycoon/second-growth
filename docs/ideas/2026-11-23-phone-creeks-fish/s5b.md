- **Opening time-lapse (`opening-lapse-check`): floods were drowning the new planting.** A probe followed every tree the player plants along the creek through the 10 years and recorded why each one died. The biggest killer was the flood event. On every day of a flood, it removed every young (growth under 0.3) flood-intolerant tree on the flooded ground outright. Bigleaf maple and paper birch are about half of the mix, so a single winter flood took a third of a fresh planting. Real riparian saplings mostly ride out a week of winter flood.
  - Fix (`updateFlood` in `js/sim/events.js`, a rule line in 0028): such a sapling now drowns at 1/8 odds per flooded day while the water stands, instead of all of them on day one. A flood lasts 4–12 days, so an 8-day flood still takes about two thirds of them.
  - I tried 1/25 first, the odds used for trees in still water. It kept more of the planting (12-seed median about 29), but in a controlled PNW balance run the restore score fell 2.5–7.5 points on all 3 seeds. The control drew the same random numbers but used the old kill. Invasive cover rose (broom, reed canarygrass, blackberry), so floods also hold weedy floodplain cover back. At 1/8 the score sits within seed noise of the control (+1.0, −0.7, −3.9; the control itself was −1.7 to +2.1 from HEAD) and invasives are mixed.

On those, beavers fell most of the planting (up to 30 of 42 trees), plus deer browse on one. See question 1.
  - Before the flood fix I tried a guess that turned out wrong: the brush planting trees whose trunks stand in the creek. It changed nothing and was reverted.
- **Serengeti hippo (`home-range-check`): a real regression, not teetering.** I bisected over this branch and creeks-fps against origin/main. The first bad commit is 9add1d3, the smooth shores commit (0054). Over six runs the hippo's water-trip share went from a median of 12% to 22%, against a ceiling of 20%. The cause: four of six hippos are herd followers with no home of their own, so their thirst ran on the 5–9 day base timer. Lying in their pool, they always had a bank spot beside them and kept stopping to "drink". Smoother shores gave them more bank spots.
  - Fix (`drinkEvery` in `js/sim/home-range.js`, a rule line in 0013): a follower in a herd that lives in its water (`move: 'semi'`) drinks at its leader's home pace. A walking herd's followers keep their own pace. My first, broader version broke `animal-positioning-check`, because elephants reaching a bank together must drink.
  - The check passes on 11 runs (RUNS 1–11).

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Beavers still fell most of the first planting on some seeds.** On about a third of seeds the beavers that move in fell 20–30 of the ~45 trees the player planted, so the time-lapse ends with 15–17 trees. Beavers felling cottonwood, alder and birch along a creek is real, and it's why restoration crews put wire guards on riparian plantings. *Recommend:* a cheap "tree guard" (a wire cage, which also stops deer) for planted saplings, offered after the first beaver cut, rather than making beavers ignore planted trees. Until then, the check passes on the median of its seeds, not on every seed.
2. **Should the opening check judge five seeds instead of three?** With about a third of seeds under 20, a three-seed median fails roughly one time in four whenever the random stream shifts. Five seeds would fail about one time in five, but add about 40 s to CI. *Recommend:* keep three seeds for now and fix the beaver question instead. That's the real cause.

## New ideas noticed

- Shrubs under a flood still die on day one when young (`shrubG < 0.35` in `updateFlood`). They should probably get the same daily odds as trees. I left it alone: it's out of scope, and willow and dogwood are wet-tolerant anyway.
- `dipper-check` on run seed 3: after the scripted 5-year Hollis restoration, the creek holds K 0.81 dippers (< 1), so the check can't bring a pair in. It's another seed-luck setup, and the check could restore for 6 years or accept K ≥ 0.8.
- The hippo is in the water most of the day but rarely shows as "in water" in the activity budget, because a follower's day is idling at the bank. Real hippos lie in the water by day and graze at night. Whether they rest in the pool or beside it would be worth a visual check.
