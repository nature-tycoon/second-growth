## Done

- **Shared fruit shapes** in `render3d/botanical.js` (`fruitHead(form, colour)`): berry cluster, drupelet berry, berry umbel, whorls, hips, ridged pod, barrels, hanging bunch, spiny, hairy, capped, long fruit, pod bunch, fig twig. Each is one geometry per shape and colour, instanced over every plant bearing it, like the flower heads.
- **Which plant gets which shape** is one short table, `fruitForm(p)` in `js/plant-patterns.js` next to the wildflower `FORMS`. Any shrub not listed gets the berry cluster; trees only show fruit where `look.hangFruit` already said so.
- **Heliconia flowers** are a new `claw` flower head (`heads()` in `botanical.js`), on both maps.
- **Cost kept to close range only**: the new shapes draw only in the close-up level of detail. From afar and at normal play zoom the pools draw the same dot as before (`'lo'` for the middle distance, like the shrub flower heads), and the number of fruit placed per plant is unchanged. Close up a fruit head is 68 to 412 triangles (the old dot was 20; a shipped umbel flower head is 320). The bushiest bunches use faceted berries to stay under that.
- Close-up before and after: `s6-fruit-before.png` and `s6-fruit-after.png` in the shared notes folder (the shapes rendered side by side at the size of a fruit head).
- Checks: `vegetation-check`, `brush-render-check`, `plant-performance-check` pass. A node sweep built all six land maps in all twelve months with every flowering and fruiting species planted. Every new fruit pool appeared (PNW 9, Atlanta 5, Serengeti 3, Amazon 2, Sumatra 10, Chinandega 4) and nothing crashed.
- **Not done: `play-profile` on two maps.** The Mac was on battery at 13% and draining, so frame timings would have meant nothing and a long GPU profile risked running it flat. Because the new shapes only draw close up and add no extra fruit, the expected cost is a few thousand triangles in the close-up view. Run `node tools/play-profile.mjs sumatra,pnw` (and against HEAD~1 with `PLAY_ROOT`) on mains power to confirm.
- Not checked in a live game view, only as models: the in-game lighting of the heliconia bracts' back faces (the flower material lights both faces, so they should look like the other petals).

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Should the fruit show at normal play zoom too, not only close up?** Recommended: no, keep it close-up only. At play zoom a fruit is a few pixels, so a dot of colour reads the same and costs nothing. Turning it on everywhere would multiply fruit triangles across the whole view.
2. **Most trees show their flowers only as a crown tint** (crepe myrtle, Callery pear, black cherry, umbrella thorn, fever tree, baobab, sausage tree, genízaro, madroño, cortés, macuelizo, madero negro). They aren't blobs, so this session left them. Should they get blossom heads like the dogwood? Recommended: yes for the showy ones (crepe myrtle, cortés, macuelizo, genízaro's pink puffs, the sausage tree's hanging flowers), using the existing `treeflower` path and shared flower heads, in a later session.
3. **Fruit trees outside Sumatra** (balanites, sycamore fig, inga, açaí, the Amazon and Chinandega figs, guácimo, jícaro, Atlanta dogwood and black cherry) show fruit only as crown colour or not at all. Add `hangFruit` to the big-fruited ones (jícaro's gourds, inga's pods, açaí's bunches)? Recommended: yes for jícaro, inga and açaí. They are big and distinctive and the shapes already exist (long fruit, pod bunch, hanging bunch).

## New ideas noticed

- The sausage tree's fruit (huge hanging sausages on long cords) is a natural showpiece for the Serengeti, and the long-fruit shape could hang on a long stalk.
- Fruit could fall: a few fallen fruits under durian, fig and mango trees in season would draw animals (the sim already has fruit eaters). That is a sim change, not just drawing.
- Elderberry flowers and berries share the umbel layout, so flowering could turn into fruiting smoothly across the month boundary instead of switching.
