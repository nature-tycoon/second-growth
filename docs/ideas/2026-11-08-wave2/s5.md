# Wave 2, session 5: Eating for every animal

## Done

- **Shared eating styles by food, not by species** (`js/render3d/eating-pose.js`, decision 0039).
  The pose is picked from the meal the simulation already has, so a new species gets one from
  what it eats:
  - **crop**: grass, seeds, insects, fruit or dung on the ground: head down, nodding with each bite.
  - **browse**: a shrub with the head about level, a tree with the head reaching up, tugging as it
    strips. Before, browsers put their heads to the ground for every meal, even at a tree.
  - **tear**: at a kill or carcass: head down, pulling back hard every few seconds.
  - **hand to mouth**: leaf-eating monkeys (howlers, Congo, langur) now use the fruit-meal arm
    pose at their leaf meals too. Fruit monkeys and apes already had it.
  - **snap**: reef fish, turtles and dugongs at a meal dart forward, nose-down at algae, coral,
    sponge or seagrass. Hunting fish snap at their catch. River fish pick at drift now and then.
  - **peck**: ground birds already pecked (0030), so no change.
- **Nibbling for animals that have no meal in the simulation:** turtles, ostriches, ground hornbills,
  beavers and ducks on land now dip their heads now and then while standing, as grazers do.
- **Drawing only.** No simulation code changed, so balance can't move and no `balance-sim` run was
  needed. `play-profile serengeti high 1`, before / after / before: animals drawing 0.25 / 0.28 /
  0.21 ms, simulation 0.9 / 0.9 / 0.8 ms. That's within noise. The laptop was on battery (18%,
  capped at 30 fps), so these numbers are rougher than usual.
- **Screenshots** (in the shared notes folder): `s5-crop.jpg`, `s5-browse.jpg`, `s5-hand.jpg`,
  `s5-fish-snap.jpg`, `s5-crop-small.jpg`. The close-up review page has new poses for each style:
  `tools/animal-closeup-review.html?pose=crop|browse|hand|snap&species=map:key,…`.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Leaf-eating monkeys hold the same gold "fruit" as fruit eaters.** The morsel is one model part
   with one colour. A green leaf would need a second part on the primate model. *Recommended:
   leave it. At game zoom it reads as "eating", and the pose is right.*
2. **Perched fruit birds (toucans, macaws, parrots, hornbills in trees) never eat visibly.** They
   need a short fruit meal at a fruiting tree, like the monkeys' meal. That's a simulation change,
   so it would need a balance check. *Recommended: yes, as a small next session. It would reuse
   `fruitAt` and the bird perch, and add a head-down peck while perched.*
3. **Sloths eat nothing visibly.** Their grips use the head slot. *Recommended: leave it. Sloths
   move so slowly that a meal pose barely reads, and freeing the slot is model work.*
4. **Seabirds (booby, noddy) don't fish.** *Recommended: a plunge-dive in the existing `fishing`
   profile, in a later reef session.*

## New ideas noticed

- Squirrels could sit up and hold a nut in both paws. Their model would need a folding-forelimb
  pose like the primates' `feedingPose`. That's model work, so I left it out.
- Grazers could leave a short-lived "grazed" mark (shorter grass) on the tile after a meal. The
  sim already lowers `groundG`, so this is drawing only.
- The close-up review page could animate its poses (nod, tug, snap) rather than show one frame.
  That would make before/after reviews of motion easier.
