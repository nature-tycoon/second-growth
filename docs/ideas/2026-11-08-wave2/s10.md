# Session 10: blossom heads and hanging fruit

Cole approved session 6's recommendations 2 and 3 (and 1: fruit stays close-up only).

## Done

- **Showy trees get real blossom heads** instead of only a tinted crown. Which tree gets which head is one table, `TREE_BLOSSOMS` / `treeBlossomForm(p)` in `js/plant-patterns.js` (next to the wildflower `FORMS` and `FRUITS`); `render3d/flora.js` places them.
  - Crepe myrtle: pink panicles (`plume`). Genízaro: pink puffs (`puff`), half again as many heads, since its umbrella crown is huge. Cortés: yellow trumpets, macuelizo: pink trumpets (`trumpet`, as the ipê). Sausage tree: a new `pendant` head in `render3d/botanical.js`, wine-red cup flowers at the end of long cords hanging under the crown.
  - The heads now sit on real leaves on the upper half of the crown, where the camera sees them. Before, they were placed inside the crown and most were hidden by the leaf blobs (this also helps the dogwood, magnolia, redbud and ipê that had heads already). Cole saw some floating off the first version, which placed them on an idealised oval: the crowns are lumpy, so over dips they hovered. Now each crown shape is measured once (`crownReach` in `flora.js`, cached on the shape): blossoms go on its outward-facing leaf points, hanging flowers on its lower edge, and hung fruit (inga, and Sumatra's durian and the rest) just inside the farthest leaf in that direction and height.
  - Head size per form (`BLOSSOM_SIZE`): a puff or panicle is drawn as big as the whole cluster, or a tree in full bloom read as bare.
- **Hanging fruit outside Sumatra**: `hangFruit` and `fruitSize` on three trees, and their `FRUITS` entries.
  - Jícaro: a new round `gourd`, on the trunk and main limbs (a new `look.fruitOnWood`; the real tree bears its gourds straight out of the wood).
  - Inga (Amazon): `pods`, round the crown like Sumatra's petai.
  - Açaí: `grape` bunches hanging just under the fronds of each stem (palms had no hung fruit before; the stem tops come from the palm's trunk data).
- **Cost** (decision 0022): full heads and fruit close up only. At play zoom the blossom heads now draw their light model and the fruit the old dot; zoomed right out a blossom head is a 4-triangle dot (`blossomDot`). ## Open questions for Cole

1. **Should the sausage tree hang its sausages too** (August to October)? Recommended: yes. They are the tree's whole identity, huge and on long cords, and the `oblong` fruit shape on a longer stalk is a small change, close-up only like the rest.
2. **Inga pods are drawn in the fruit colour the data gives them, a pale cream** (the pulp). Real ice-cream bean pods are green to yellow-green and ridged. Recommended: change the inga's `berry` to a yellow-green, which also changes the colour its fruit shows from afar.
3. **The rest of s6's crown-tint list** (Callery pear, black cherry, umbrella thorn, fever tree, baobab, madroño, madero negro) still shows bloom as crown colour only. Recommended: leave them. They are less showy, or (baobab, at night) seen rarely, and each tree with heads adds triangles close up.

## New ideas noticed

- Fallen blossom: macuelizo and cortés drop a carpet of pink or yellow under the tree at the end of their bloom; a few petal patches on the ground (a ground decal, no geometry per flower) would be cheap and striking in the dry season.
- Hummingbirds and bees could visit the blossom heads in 3D (the pollinators already know nectar months for genízaro, cortés and macuelizo).
- Jícaro gourds are eaten by horses and cattle when they fall; a fallen-fruit food source (s6's idea) fits it well.
