# Wave 2, session 4: Rich hunts

## Done

- **One shared pounce.** Cats (cougar, bobcat, lion, leopard, jaguar, tiger, ocelot and the rest of
  the `stalk` style) end a rush with a short leap at where the prey will be when they land: nose up
  as they spring, body stretched with the forelegs reaching and the hind legs trailing, nose down as
  they come down on it. The cheetah's version is a low, fast lunge from closer in (its trip-and-grab).
  Foxes and coyotes leap only at small prey (4 kg and under: rabbits, voles, chipmunks, mice), with
  a high mousing arc that dives nose-first. It is one trait on the hunt style (`pounce`), not a
  per-species patch: a new cat or fox gets it from its kind.
- **A miss is a stumble.** A leap that comes down short (the prey moved off its line) ends in a
  stumble, then the chase goes on while the hunter has wind, or it lets the prey go (`letGo`), as
  any missed grab does.
- **Wolves, wild dogs and hyenas don't leap.** They run prey down as before, and the grab now reads:
  the head and forequarters drive in and the head drops as they close the last body length.
- **Extra touch:** a **dust puff** (a splash in shallow water) where the hunter springs and where it
  lands. I also built a prey **jink** (cutting sideways after a missed grab) and took it out again:
  it let far more rabbits get away, which changes balance (open question 1).
- Catch odds are unchanged: landing on the prey triggers the usual catch roll.
- Checks: `predation-soak 12` kill rates moved within the seed spread (lion 1.5 → 1.8, cougar 1.9 →
  1.9, fox 6.6 → 5.6, cheetah 3.8 → 2.9, jaguar 2.1 → 3.6; hunters that don't pounce moved as much).
  Seeded `balance-sim restore 15`, year 15, seeds 7/11/23: score 64.7/65.3/62.6 → 60.9/64.3/62.8,
  natural 54/55/53% → 49/54/50%, rabbits as before. `hunt-check` passes on seeds 91 and 5; on its

  code is unchanged and the old code shows the same short cheetah stalks on seed 5 (0.58), so it's
  that seed taking another path, not the pounce. animal-life, prey-avoidance, animal-behavior and
  challenging-ecology checks pass. `play-profile serengeti high 2`: 60 fps in the play, wide and
  close views before and after, drawing animals 0.2 ms both. The top-speed view varied run to run
  on both versions (30–60 fps, plant drawing 0.4–3.8 ms) with the machine busy (load 3.6–6.6) and

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Should prey jink?** Real rabbits, gazelles and deer dodge sideways when a hunter closes in, and
   that dodge is how many of them escape. With it, PNW brush rabbits recovered (year 15: 6, 0, 14 →
   19, 15, 19 over three seeds) and hawks and coyotes caught less. *Recommended:* yes, in a later
   session, with predators' catch odds raised a little so kills per year stay where balance has them.
2. **The cheetah stalk limit in hunt-check fails on its default seed** (see Done). *Recommended:*
   move hunt-check to three seeds (1987, 91, 5) and judge the median across them, as 0026's own
   tables do, rather than loosen the limit.
3. **Should a pounce sometimes come down short on purpose?** Now about 1 leap in 30 misses, only
   when the prey moves off the predicted line. Real cat pounces miss more often. *Recommended:* leave
   it: making leaps miss more would cut kills per year unless the catch odds go up to match.
