# Session 6: deer upgrades (solo pass, for Cole's review)

Idea 8: upgrade the other deer the way the black-tailed deer was (aesthetics s7). Done solo with the
animal-model checklist: each step judged against side-on Commons photos in the overlay page, steps
that didn't look closer dropped. Drawing only: no simulation change, no new shader program.

How it's built: the white-tailed deer, elk and sambar now use the black-tailed deer's fitted build
(`slender: true` on their sprites) with per-species sprite fields that default to the black-tailed
deer's values, so the black-tailed deer is byte-identical (its geometry hashed before and after every
step). The mouse-deer is a chevrotain with its own builder (`mouseDeer`, agouti kind) and was refitted
on its own.

Pictures in `s6/`: `<key>-<model>-<view>-before.jpg` (base commit e51e8f6) and `-after.jpg`, males as
`-before-male` / `-after-male`; `close` is the close-up model, `detail` the usual one. Views: `side`,
`quarter`, `head`, `headside`, `front`, `walk`, `golden`, `game`, and (after only, new) `rear`.
Keys: `deer` = white-tailed deer, `elk`, `sambar`, `mousedeer`.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Male mouse-deer tusks.** Males have small upper canines showing below the lip. Drawing them needs a
   male variant for the species (`sprite.male`), which also switches on the simulation's male-variant
   rules for it and adds a second model to draw, for a 2–3 mm tooth on a rabbit-sized animal.
   *Recommendation:* leave them out.
2. **Bull elk's mane** (and the sambar stag's). The bull photos show a shaggy dark throat. A deeper,
   rougher underside on the male neck would do it, a step of its own. *Recommendation:* yes, as one small
   follow-up step, checked against the bull photos.
3. **Elk ears.** Sized from the cow's head-in-profile photo, they're as large as the cow's (about 0.6 of
   the nose-to-ear distance); from the side they look big next to a field-guide picture.
   *Recommendation:* keep (the photo decides); trim 10% if they read too big in play.
4. **Chinandega's white-tailed deer** got the same settings as Atlanta's (same species; the Central
   American race is smaller, which its sprite size already gives). *Recommendation:* keep.
