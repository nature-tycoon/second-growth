# Session 7: cats 1, the shared cat build and the small cats (solo pass, for Cole's review)

Idea 9, part 1: carry the cougar's approved fixes into the code every cat shares, then fit the bobcat,
ocelot and leopard cat to photos. Done with the animal-model checklist, each step judged against Commons
photos in the overlay page (`tools/animal-photo-overlay.html`), steps that didn't look closer dropped.
Drawing only: no simulation change, no new shader program. Cole's newest cougar pass (branch
`claude/cougar-model-refinements-0afa33`, approved) was merged in first (8a21aa9), at Cole's word to
build the cats on it.

How it's built: every `feline` but the lion now uses the cougar's fitted build (face strips and pads,
painted eye `catEye`, face fur `catFace`, legs, paws, tail form) with per-species sprite fields that
default to the cougar's values (`legs`, `legR0`, `legR1`, `headSize`, `eyeSize`, `ear`, `ears`,
`earSpot`, `tailLen`, `tailR`, `tailDrop`, `tailTip`, `calm`...), so the cougar is byte-identical (its
geometry hashed after every step, both models). The lion keeps its own head (built round the mane) and
the tiger its own builder: both byte-identical too, their pictures unchanged.

Pictures in `s7/`: `<key>-<model>-<view>-before.jpg` (8a21aa9, the cougar merge) and `-after.jpg`;
`close` is the close-up model, `detail` the usual one. Views: `side`, `quarter`, `head`, `headside`,
`front`, `walk`, `golden`, `rear`, `game`. Keys: `bobcat`, `ocelot` (Amazon; the Chinandega ocelot is
the same build), `leopardcat`, and the other cats `jaguar`, `leopard`, `lion`, `cheetah`, `tiger`,
`clouded`. The ocelot lives on two maps: Amazon and Chinandega.

### Cole's three looks this session

1. "The faces look like bad drawings and too angry": the cougar's serious brow (slanting down to its
   inner end) and down-turned mouth read as a scowl on the others. New field `calm` (0 the cougar).
2. "The dots were fine, it's that the faces looked drawn on, almost like imposed human faces": at
   `calm: 1` there's no mouth line and the brow is only a faint level ridge with no colour (a lid, so
   the eye doesn't bulge); the pale fur goes round the eye instead of over it as a pale eyebrow. Head
   spots back as they were. All cats but the cougar are at 1 (the jaguar 0.8).
3. "The bobcat's markings look smudged" / "make sure all of the animals have the right face markings":
   marks painted in the head's vertex colours blurred across its vertices. Each species' marks are now
   strips laid on the head (`catMarks` → `surfaceStrip`, as the cougar's tear marks are): crisp, with
   edges in the fur's own colour. Face marks by species:
   - bobcat: dark bars across the cheek ruff, dark streaks up the forehead, no head spots;
   - ocelot: two black stripes back along each cheek, a black line from each eye up the forehead;
   - leopard cat: a white streak from each eye's inner corner up the forehead between dark lines, dark
     cheek bars;
   - clouded leopard: black cheek stripes and forehead lines;
   - cheetah: its black tear line from the eye to the mouth, now a strip on the head (was a rod);
   - leopard, jaguar: small spots on the head (as before), the tear mark from the eye.
