# Session 5: where players drop out (PostHog)

Cole asked: track usage so we can see where people drop out of the game and what to improve. He
chose new events, a dashboard and a report.

**Dashboard: [Player drop-off](https://us.posthog.com/project/630638/dashboard/2423211)** (testers
left out). Event list and what it can and can't answer: [docs/posthog-events.md](../../posthog-events.md).

## The report (PostHog, 2026-11-21 → 11-27, since the farm opening went live)

Small numbers: 54 first visits in a week (48 PNW). Read them as directions, not rates.

**First visits** (funnel tile "First visit: opening funnel"): 54 start → 37 see an animal (69%) →
22 tear out the fence (41%) → 16 pull the culvert (30%) → 9 get the time-lapse (17%) → 6 reach
5 active minutes (11%). Of the 48 on PNW, 6 reached 10 active minutes and 3 came back another day.
The intro film: 34 started, 23 skipped it, 10 watched to the end; it isn't where they leave.

**All players by device** (since 11-21): desktop 51 opened the page, 48 loaded, 40 used a tool, 9
reached 10 minutes, 7 came back. Phones: only 7 real phone visits; 2 of them left on "Catching the
light" (shader warm-up, the last loading step), and 3 of the phone exits were under 30 s with no tool.

**Where sessions end** (`game_left`, last per player): on desktop 23 of 44 left within 2 active
minutes, 9 of those without a single tool, the mode choice ("How do you want to play?") the most
common open panel for returning players. 

**Loading**: 4 players left while loading, one desktop player hit a broken load 16 times, one
lost the 3D view. And one iPhone player whose page apparently dies: see 3.

### Top 3 drop-off points and suggested fixes

1. **The fence prompt (biggest).** 59% of first visits never tear out the fence. Ten of them
   watched the intro, saw the deer at the fence and left within a minute; several brushed 2–10
   tool strokes instead of pressing the button (so they saw the tools, not the prompt), and a few
   ran at 8–18 fps. *Fix:* make the fence the one thing to do: hide the tool bar (or dim it) until
   the fence is out or × is pressed, make the button larger with a soft pulse, and let a tap on
   the glowing fence itself tear it out. The new `opening_prompt` and fence `skipped` events will
   show whether they saw it and closed it, or never saw it.
2. **The guided planting before the time-lapse.** Of 22 who opened the fence, 12 planted the trees
   and the meadow and 9 got to the time-lapse, the game's payoff. The strokes (`TREES`, `MEADOW`
   in `ui/opening-plan.js`) take effort before anything rewards them. *Fix:* fewer required
   plants per step (half), the finger hint at 8 s instead of 20 s, and the time-lapse starting
   after the trees if the meadow stalls for 30 s. Lowering `TREES` trips the payoff check, so
   that's a tuning session with `opening-lapse-check`, not a one-line change.
3. **iPhone pages that die while loading.** One iPhone player (428×926, a reef farm, arriving
   from ChatGPT) tried about 25 times from 11-24 to 11-27. Each try left one event, the "Welcome
   back" panel at 82% of loading, under a new PostHog id, with no page view, no `loading_left`, no
   `game_loaded`. That fits iOS Safari killing the page for memory during the last loading steps
   (shaders), which leaves nothing. It also makes that one person ~25 "players" in every count.
   *Fix:* the new `page_crashed` event sizes it from the next visit; then, on a crash, start the
   next load on Fast at a lower resolution, and measure the reef's memory on the iOS Simulator.

After the opening, the next loss is between the time-lapse and coming back (12 watched it, 3 of 48
returned). That needs the new `play_milestone` data before saying more.

## Done

- **Audit** of every event (about 70 kinds; the table is in docs/posthog-events.md) and the gaps:
  no record of whether an opening prompt was ever seen; × on the fence and culvert prompts sent
  nothing; no clean play-time steps (heartbeats only every 5 min); `game_left` said what was open
  but not the last panel, tool, opening step or how smoothly it ran; and a page killed by the
  browser left no trace at all.
- **New events**, few, all game state, no personal data:
  - `opening_prompt { step, map, seconds }` as each opening prompt first shows (`ui/opening.js`
    `shown`), and `opening_step { step: fence|culvert, how: skipped }` for × on those two.
  - `play_milestone { minutes: 1|3|10|30, map, first_visit, opening_stage, last_tool, last_panel,
    fps_band, quality, tools_session, rewards_session, ... }` once each per page load.
  - `page_crashed { stage, map, seconds, minutes_ago }`: the page keeps a breadcrumb in
    localStorage while on screen (`noteCrumb`, cleared on hide and `pagehide`, never written after
    `pagehide`); one found at the next start was a page that died (`js/analytics.js`, `js/main.js`).
  - `game_loaded` gets `first_visit`; `game_left` and `went_idle` get `first_visit`,
    `opening_stage`, `last_tool`, `last_panel`, `fps_band`, `quality` (`SessionAnalytics.where`).
  - Not added, because existing events already answer them: first use of each tool (`tool_used`),
    first animal (`first_animal_seen`), year N (`year_reached`), panel opened (`panel_opened`).
- **PostHog** (project 630638, created only insights and one dashboard): "Player drop-off" with
  the first-visit opening funnel, all players load → 5 minutes by device, daily return retention,
  loading trouble (weekly), play time reached by device (new events), the opening prompts
  shown/closed/done table (new events), and exits under 2 minutes by open panel.
- **Checks**: `tools/analytics-privacy-check.mjs` extended (the breadcrumb stores and sends only
  stage, map and two numbers; stale, unreadable and cleared ones send nothing), passes.
  `tools/play-analytics-browser-check.mjs` extended (play_milestone, opening_prompt, the fence ×,
  page_crashed from a dead page's breadcrumb and none after a normal close, game_left's new
  fields), passes. Headless suite (157 scripts), `opening-lapse-check` and
  `first-minutes-browser-check` pass. Not seen live yet: the events reach PostHog only from the
  next release.
- Docs: docs/posthog-events.md (new, linked from AGENTS.md and posthog-player-perf.md).

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Should the fence be the only thing to do until it's out?** (hide or dim the tool bar, bigger
   pulsing button, tap the fence itself.) *Recommended: yes*; it's the biggest loss, and it
   doesn't change the ecology, only the first 30 seconds.
2. **Fewer plants in the guided planting, and the time-lapse sooner?** *Recommended: halve the
   strokes and start the time-lapse after the trees if the meadow stalls*, tuned against the
   payoff check in its own session.
3. **After a crash, open the next visit on Fast at lower resolution and say so?** *Recommended:
   yes, but only once `page_crashed` has a week of data*, so we know it's more than one phone.

## New ideas noticed

- An alert on `page_crashed` (more than 3 players a day) would catch a release that breaks
  phones. Cheap to add once the event has a baseline.
- The mode choice ("How do you want to play?") is still the most common panel open when returning
  players leave in under 2 minutes. A returning player could land straight in their last farm
  with the choice in the menu, as first visits now do.
- The "Welcome back" iPhone case also means PostHog loses that player's id each time. Worth a
  look at why the SDK's storage doesn't stick there (private mode? storage cleared by the crash?).
- `game_left.fps_band` by `opening_stage` will show whether slow frame rates are what lose
  first visits at the fence; if they are, the opening view could start at a cheaper zoom.
