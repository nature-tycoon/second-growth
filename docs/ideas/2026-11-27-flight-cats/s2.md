# Session 2: Bird flight (ideas 3, 4)

## Done

- **Landing flare** (idea 3). Through the last stretch down to its perch, the ground or the water, a landing bird keeps its wings open and beats them hard to brake. Its body pitches nose up (0.5 rad), and in the last half tile its legs swing forward to take the ground. A duck landing on water comes in feet first and tucks its feet once it's down. Measured with `tools/bird-flight-check.mjs` over about 1000 seeded landings on pnw and atlanta. In the last 0.3 tiles, wings were open in 93% of frames (1–13% before) and beating in 100%. 90–97% of landings flared nose up.
- **Take-off.** It already had wingbeats. Now a climbing bird beats harder and lifts its nose a little.
- **Banked turns for every bird**, eased and capped by flight style. Roll jolt (99th percentile) is about 0.02 per frame, below the old soaring hawk's 0.03. Circling soarers keep 0012's lean exactly.
- **Flight style by trait** (`flightStyle` in `js/data/animal-kinds.js`, no species names):
  - `soar`: raptors, vultures, secretary bird, owls, hornbills. In travel flight they beat a few times, then glide on level wings (about 40% of cruising frames). The hawk's own circling is unchanged.
  - `bound`: songbirds, woodpeckers, toucans. A burst of about three beats, then wings folded in a shallow dip (about 0.07 tiles, under one body length). Wings are folded in about a third of cruising frames.
  - `swoop`: the passing barn swallow flock. It weaves a little faster (sim passage only, no ecology) and banks up to 0.31 rad. Geese bank 0.
  - `steady`: ducks, geese, herons, cranes, boobies, parrots, hummingbirds (the default). Steady beats, banks of 0.25 rad at most.
- 0039's steady flight still holds: the dip is drawn on top of the steady line, and `motion-smoothness-check` measures the line without it. Everything runs on the sim's phase, so pause freezes it.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **How deep should a small bird's bounding dip be?** It's now about 0.7 of a body length from top to bottom. Real finches and woodpeckers dip about that much or more. *Recommend:* keep it, and deepen it only if it reads as too subtle when you watch a robin cross the meadow.
2. **Should resident terns (the reef's brown noddy) also swoop?** They have the default `steady` style for now. Making them swoop would change their passage flights' speed and path. *Recommend:* yes, as a one-line follow-up: `flightStyle: 'swoop'` on the noddy.
3. **Should the landing also slow the bird horizontally?** The flare is drawing only, so a bird still arrives at its sim speed. Most landings are short and look right, but a fast flier coming in from far can look like it brakes with its wings while still gliding in. *Recommend:* leave it. Easing the sim's last tile would change the sim's timings and need a balance run.
