shared cat build from s7. Done with the animal-model checklist: each step was judged against Commons

closer were dropped. Drawing only: no simulation change and no new shader program. The cougar stays
byte-identical: its geometry was hashed after every step, on both models.

`front`, `walk`, `golden`, `rear`, `game`. Keys: `jaguar`, `leopard`, `tiger`, `clouded`.

**Jaguar** (Amazon; there's no jaguar on the Chinandega map). Pictures: `s8/jaguar-*`.
- Changed:
  - Long and low: `h` 17 → 13.5, `leg` 14 → 11.5.
  - A bigger, broad head (`headSize` 1.3) with a shorter muzzle, carried lower and closer in (new `headX`, `headY`).
  - A slight belly tuck, and a shorter tail carried higher.
  - Rosettes smaller and denser (`rosette` 0.72) and redrawn irregular: four to six blotches of varied size round a darker centre with a dot or two. The old ones looked like one stamped symbol.

- Would still change: the body is still a plain barrel, without a jaguar's heavy shoulders. The eyes were hard to settle: 1.35 stared, 1.1 looked buried, so I settled on 1.2.
- Photos: Jaguar (Panthera onca palustris) male Three Brothers River, and ... River 2 (Charles J. Sharp, CC BY-SA 4.0).

**Leopard** (Serengeti). Pictures: `s8/leopard-*`.

  - Lower: `h` 14 → 11, `leg` 12 → 9.5.
  - A smaller head (0.95) set a little lower and closer in.
  - The long tail hangs steeper.

**Sumatran tiger** (Sumatra). Pictures: `s8/tiger-*`. The before pictures are the old builder.
- Changed:
  - Its old builder is gone. It had bead eyes and a ruff, brows, pads and chin as separate pieces, the kind Cole calls "stuck on".
  - It now uses the cat build with the old stripe shaders: `coatTiger` on the body over rust-to-white shading, and legs barred outside and white inside.
  - A ringed tail with a black tip.
  - The calm face: white cheeks back to the ruff (`cheekPale`, `cheekSize`) and fuller cheeks, white marks over the eyes, and black stripes on the cheeks and forehead laid on as strips.
  - Round ears, black behind with a white spot.
  - A bigger head, and the tail hanging to just above the ground.
- Against the photos: the topline, tail base and legs sit on the Berlin tiger's outline. That tiger has its head turned, so the head was judged by eye.
- Would still change:
  - The old builder's rolling shoulder blades and white ruff fringe are lost; the ruff is now only fuller cheeks.
  - The face's white is softer than a real Sumatran tiger's bold white cheeks.
  - The pale inner legs end in a hard line.
- Cost: usual model 16k → 24k triangles, close-up 39k → 58k, the same as every fitted cat. Shading bench: about 1.7 ms, against 1.9 for the clouded leopard and 1.4 for the cougar, so within the cat range.
- Photos: Sumatran Tiger Berlin Tierpark (Captain Herbert, CC BY-SA 3.0); for the face, Felidae Panthera tigris sumatrae 3 (NasserHalaweh, CC BY-SA 4.0).

**Sunda clouded leopard** (Sumatra). Pictures: `s8/clouded-*`.
- Changed:
  - A long, low skull with a longer muzzle (`snout` 1.25, new `headTall` 0.88). The head photo's forehead runs straight down to the nose, and the model's was domed.

  - The clouds: broad pale lanes between them, a thick black rim and a fill well darker than the coat, with wavy edges. Before, they were a net of thin black lines.

  - The only clean side-on body photo is small and cut off at the rump. It's also a mainland clouded leopard; no side-on Sunda photo is on Commons. It shows a taller-standing cat than the brief's "short legs", so the legs moved only a little.

- Would still change: the cheek stripes still read as faint grooves rather than black stripes. It's the same strip code that made the bobcat's crisp, so it's worth a look. The big spots on the cheeks hide the stripes.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **The tiger's old shoulder blades and ruff fringe.** Moving to the shared build lost them, in exchange for a face that isn't stuck on. *Recommendation:* keep the shared build. If the tiger looks too plain beside the photos, add the ruff fringe as a laid-on strip, not a separate sheet.
2. **The jaguar and leopard are now lower and smaller-looking in the world** (same length, about a fifth less height). *Recommendation:* keep it, because it matches the photos. If they read too small in play, raise `scale` (drawing) rather than `h`.
3. **Clouded leopard leg length.** The brief said short legs, but the one usable side-on photo shows a cat standing fairly tall. *Recommendation:* leave it at the small step taken (`legs` 0.92) until a side-on Sunda photo turns up.
4. **`coatTigerHead` is now unused** (the old tiger face shader). *Recommendation:* delete it in a cleanup pass. It costs nothing at runtime but shader text.

## New ideas noticed

- The cat build could carry a tail-tip curl (a leopard's and a tiger's hanging tail turns up at the end) as one more tail point.
- Cheek strips on the clouded leopard and tiger render as grooves: check how `surfaceStrip` places strokes at `hr[2] * 1.2` on heads made taller or flatter (`headTall`, `cheeks`).
- The overlay page could take marks from a thumbnail of known size (again: every mark here was estimated off 500 px thumbnails).
- A shared "heavy shoulders" body option (a jaguar's and a tiger's) for the cat build.
