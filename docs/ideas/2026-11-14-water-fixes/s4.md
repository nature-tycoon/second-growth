# Session 4: Animals at the water surface

## Done

- **Animals no longer look like they swim on top of the water in watch mode.** Cole's note was right:
  it was every animal in the water, at eye level only. The heights were already correct (the river
  dolphin's back and head break the surface; the beaver floats with its back out). The cause was the
  tracking lens (0040): the watch camera follows its animal, so the lens is on, and the lens draws the
  animal a second time "through cover". Water writes no depth, so that second draw showed the whole
  body through the surface. Now the lens leaves out what's under the creek, river or pond at the
  animal while the eye is above it, and the water draws over it as usual (`uLookWater` in
  `render3d/focus.js`, `look-through.js`, set in `Renderer.updateFocus`).
  - Seen in the game at eye level: before, the dolphin was drawn whole and bright on the water; with
    the lens's second draw switched off, and then with the fix, only its back and head show. The beaver
    on a Hollis pond shows just its back, its body faint under the water.
  - Nothing to do with session 2's pond levels: both animals sat at the right height for the water.
- **Coho spin (motion-sweep).** Already on main: the same failure at d626592, before session 2, so the
  salmon/creek change didn't cause it. A coho arrives, turns round to face upstream (as resting fish do),
  then sets off back downstream a third of a second later. Both half-turns went the same way round, so it
  was drawn spinning a full circle. Now an about-face straight after another goes back the way the last
  one came (`turnToward` in `actors-common.js`, rule added to 0025). My first try alternated on every
  about-face, which made circling hawks and guacos spin, so it only applies within about a second.
- Checks: `motion-sweep-check` passes on every map (seeds 1987,91 and 7,42), as do `motion-smoothness-check`,
  `watch-check`, `canopy-support-check` (new check: the lens leaves out underwater parts at eye level,
  and only there) and `lens-depth-browser-check`. 0040, 0025 and AGENTS.md updated.
