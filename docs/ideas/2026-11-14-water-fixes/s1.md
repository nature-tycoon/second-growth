# Session 1: UI and settings

## Done
- **Continue button**: now a filled, larger primary button ("Continue at Hollis Farm →") with space above and below, so it sits clear of the highlighted farm box. Checked at 812×375 and desktop.
- **Sandbox: unlimited money**: a choice on the new-game screen. Saved in the farm's `flags.sandbox`. While on, `canAfford` is always true and `spend`/`pay`/`earn`/`grant` change nothing and book nothing (ledger, spent, earned stay empty); undo refunds nothing; the money display shows ∞. Analytics context, feedback snapshots and shared-farm snapshots carry `sandbox: true` so they can be filtered out; the `game_start` event is tagged too. Normal games are unchanged (0001).
- **Invasives toggle bug**: the setting was fine in a new game, but loading a saved farm (Continue) built a fresh `Game` whose `invasives = true` and copied it over the live one, turning invasives back on. `restoreSnapshot` now keeps the player's setting. With it off, no invasive seedling takes and existing ones do not spread (headless: count flat over 4 years).
- New headless check `tools/sandbox-check.mjs` (sandbox money, saved flag, setting kept on load, no spread).
