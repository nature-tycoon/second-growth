# Session 4: movement glitches, shared rules

## Done

- **Legs follow the ground, for every walker.** The leg clock ticked at walking speed even when an animal barely moved, so an elk braking behind its herd or edging round a trunk strode on the spot. Now the drawing measures how far each walker really moved and slows and shortens its stride to match. Under about a third of its pace it stands with a slight shuffle (`js/render3d/stride.js`, used in `actors.js`). Only the drawing changed, so the simulation and headless results stay the same.
  - Walkers drawn striding at under a fifth of their pace: 11% of walking frames before, about 1% after (pnw and serengeti, through the real `Actors`).
  - Cost: a few arithmetic operations per visible walker per frame, no allocation.
- **Why elk crawl.** `tools/gait-pace-probe.mjs` shows elk held up on about half their walking steps, most of any species. They are mostly braking behind a herd mate in single file (`spacing.steer`), which is real. The fix there is the stride rule above. One small cause fixed in the simulation: a herd follower that stalls near the end of its walk (a mate or trunk on its spot) now stops there, instead of pushing on the spot for up to 3 s before giving up.
- **Smoother flying over trees, for every flier.** A bird now looks about a second of its own flight ahead (1.4 to 4 tiles, sampled every 0.7 tiles so no crown is skipped). It eases up over trees and sinks back more slowly, so a forest is one smooth swell, not a hop per crown. Landings, stoops and dives keep their quick descent. The sudden changes in climb rate (`jolt` in `tools/flight-canopy-check.mjs`, new) dropped about 2 to 4 times on all four forested maps. Birds inside crowns stay under the 1% limit (0.5 to 0.73%, before 0.07 to 0.67%).
- **Salmon leap check:** `tools/animal-locate-check.mjs` now expects the new 0.3 tile-height leap (camera range over 0.2, was 0.5). The code was right; the check was stale. All 5 locate checks pass.
- Checks: `animal-locate-check` passes. `flight-canopy-check` passes, with the new jolt limit (HEAD fails it on 3 of 4 maps). `animal-steering-check` passes on every map except Atlanta with the default seed 1987. That failure (7.02 antler racks in trunks per 1000) is identical on HEAD, so it came before this session. With seed 91, Atlanta and pnw both pass.
- Balance: unseeded 5-year `balance-sim restore` before and after shows only run-to-run noise (one run had a fire); the follower rule moves herd positions, not populations.
- Decisions updated: 0025 (stride follows the ground) and 0030 (flight clearance easing).

## New ideas noticed

- `gait-pace-probe` shows other species held up as often as elk: impala, zebra, giraffe and elephant around 55 to 70% of walking steps in the Serengeti. The stride rule hides this visually, but herds there may be moving slower than intended.
- Amazon kingfishers make up all of the Amazon's in-crown samples (fishing from perches inside trees). A perch-exit rule for fishers would tidy that.
