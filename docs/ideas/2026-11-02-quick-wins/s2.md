# Session 2: Settings and keystone camera

## Done

- **Invasive species setting** (Settings → Gameplay, saved with the other settings). Off: no invasive seedling takes root, from the map edges or from plants already on the land (`w.invasiveOdds = 0` in `updatePlants`, which every seedling goes through in `trySeed`). Invasives already on the land **stay** until the player clears them; the setting stops arrivals and spread, it doesn't wipe the map.
- **Keystone moments setting** (same place). Off: no camera takeover, no title card; the moment becomes an ordinary notification (in the journal, clickable to look), so the player still hears that it happened.
- **Moments from eye level / watch mode.** The cause: at eye level the eye camera stayed on, so the overhead camera flew to the moment where nobody could see it. Now the moment leaves the eye camera, and once it ends watch mode goes back to eye level with a new animal.
- **Autumn rut didn't zoom at 3x.** Cause found: a click on an animal during the moment started following that animal, which stops the camera's glide in. Speed itself was not the cause (the moment drops to normal speed and glides the same at the fastest speed). Fix: during a moment, clicks on the map do nothing; Continue or Esc ends it (Continue's tooltip now says "Back to your view (Esc)"). The check reproduced the bug with the guard removed and passes with it.
- New check `tools/moment-camera-browser-check.mjs`: glide at the fastest speed with an animal clicked mid-glide, from eye level and back, moments off, invasives off. Passes. Note in decision 0020.

## Open questions for Cole

1. With invasives off, should the invasives already on the land also disappear? Recommended: **no**, they stay and you clear them as usual. Removing them would erase most of each map's restoration work (and its goals), which would make the game much easier than "Relaxed".
2. With keystone moments off, is a plain notification okay, or should they be completely silent? Recommended: **keep the notification**. It's small, can be hidden with the Notifications setting, and you'd otherwise never know the rut or the salmon run happened.
3. Should a click during a moment still do something (for example, show the animal's info without moving the camera)? Recommended: **no**, keep it simple. Continue/Esc is the way out.

## New ideas noticed

- The frame loop caps each frame's time at 0.1 s, so on a slow computer (or a heavy frame) the moment's 2.4 s glide stretches out in real time (7–19 s in software rendering here). It still gets there. Moving the glide onto real time would make it the same length everywhere.
- Changing either setting has no on-screen reminder in the game. A small "Invasives off" tag in the Ecosystem Report would explain why the map stays clean.
