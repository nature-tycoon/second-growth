# Session 3: canopy variety, Serengeti soil, reef

Items 3 and 4 of the [aesthetics plan](../../aesthetics-plan-2026-11-26.md). Instance colours, map
colours, the terrain atlas and one line in the shared sea patch: no new shader programs, no geometry.

Pictures: `s3/<map>-<midday|golden>-<before|after>.jpg` (before = s2's after, commit 990bd1a), same
camera as s2 (`node tools/value-capture.mjs <dir> amazon sumatra serengeti reef`). Brightness
percentiles in `values-before.json` / `values-after.json`.

## Done

- **Canopy variety.** Each tree crown's colour now varies by up to ±8.5% in value and a few degrees of
  hue (warmer/yellower or cooler/bluer) by default, ±12% and more hue in the rainforests, 60% of that
  for conifers (`crownVary` in `js/render3d/flora.js`, `look.crownVary` per map). It's the instance
  colour, so it reaches the far crown cards (0042) with no rebake and no cost per frame.
- **Species spread** (Amazon, Sumatra, `js/data/plants-*.js`): emergents greyer and paler (Brazil nut,
  kapok, meranti, keruing, tualang), figs and understorey shrubs darker and bluer (fig, mangosteen,
  piper, psychotria, cacao, clidemia, ixora), and one crown in four of mahogany, ingá, meranti and
  cempedak in yellow-green new leaf (`morphs`).
- **Serengeti's dark patches** were the fireball lily: under a fern or lily the ground is drawn as
  forest-floor duff (dark brown with moss), and on Serengeti that was multiplied by the red soil tint,
  so it came out near-black. A map can now give that ground its own colour (`look.litter`: Serengeti's
  red laterite with dry grass, `#9a7a62`, untinted; `js/render/sprites.js`, `js/render3d/terrain.js`),
  and only where the lilies grow thick (cover > 0.3), so a patch frays into the grass instead of
  ending in a stamped edge. Darkest 5% of the midday picture: 34 → 99 of 255.
- **Reef.** Caustics fade as their lines shrink toward a pixel (zoomed out or far off) down to 30%,
  and a little faster with depth (`atmosphere.js`, from `fwidth`, no new uniform): the bright mesh at
  play zoom is gone, a soft shimmer stays close up. Shallow and deep sea colours a few % deeper;
  corals and sponges +20% saturation (`look.crownVary.sat`). Reef median 165 → 160.
- Sumatra's light end rose (95th percentile 104 → 121 at midday): the pale emergents give the dark
  forest a light band, which s2 asked for.

Checks: `bare-soil-check`, `perf-guard-check`, `impostor-check`, `species-name-count` pass;
`first-frame-profile` 27 programs (unchanged code paths: the sea change is inside the shared patch);
`flora-rebuild-bench` passes with new signatures (expected: crown colours changed; PNW's too, since
the default variation widened); `horizon-browser-check` and `impostor-browser-check` pass.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **How varied should rainforest crowns be?** Now clearly mixed: grey emergents, dark blue-green
   figs, a few yellow-green crowns in new leaf. *Recommendation:* keep. If it reads too busy, drop
   `crownVary.hue` in `js/maps/amazon.js` / `sumatra.js` from 0.045 to 0.03; if the new-leaf crowns
   look like disease, remove the `morphs` on mahogany, ingá, meranti and cempedak.
2. **Temperate maps changed a little too** (default ±8.5% instead of ±4.5%; conifers ±5%). Not
   photographed this session. *Recommendation:* keep; s4–s5 pictures of PNW and Atlanta will show it.
3. **Serengeti laterite strength.** A first try (`#a8724e`) read loud orange; the kept `#9a7a62` is
   soft red-brown under the grass. A pale pinkish ring still shows round each patch (the ground
   shader's two-pick edge: grass texture under the laterite's colour). *Recommendation:* keep;
   softening the ring needs the ground shader's edge blend changed for every map (all bare soil
   edges), which is a bigger, riskier change than this pass.
4. **Patch shapes.** Patch shapes still follow where the lilies grow (the world's tiles); I only made
   their thin edges fray. *Recommendation:* leave the sim alone; it's living ground now, not stamps.
5. **Reef still pale green overall.** The sea's own colour at play zoom is mostly the sand seen
   through shallow water. *Recommendation:* revisit in s4 with the light (a slightly lower `sunI`
   underwater or a deeper `shallow`), not more here.

## New ideas noticed

- PNW's fern floors and Atlanta's lilies in open meadow also draw as forest duff; `look.litter` (or
  duff only under canopy) would fix any that read as dark squares in the open.
- Coral `morphs` could get a wider, brighter set (purple, pink, green-tipped) now that saturation is
  lifted; the reef's coral colours are mostly tan.
