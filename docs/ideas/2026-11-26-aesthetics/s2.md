# Session 2: value and colour pass per map

Item 2 of the [aesthetics plan](../../aesthetics-plan-2026-11-26.md). Colours and constants only: each
map's `look` in `js/maps/*.js` and the pasture row of `js/render3d/palettes.js`. No shader, formula or
geometry change, so the horizon's backdrop follows the new grade by itself (`screenColor` unchanged, 0058).

Pictures: `s2/<map>-<midday|golden>-<before|after>.jpg`, the release check's forest scene (High) at
the usual zoom (0.62), midday (0.4 of the day) and golden hour (0.79). Retake them with
`node tools/value-capture.mjs <dir> [maps]`, which also prints how the picture's brightness spreads
(`values-before.json`, `values-after.json` here).

## Done

- **Grade** (every map): contrast 1.05–1.08 → 1.09–1.13 (rainforests lowest, since contrast round the
  mid-grey pivot darkens a dark map further; Serengeti highest), lift cut to 40–50%, saturation
  +0.01–0.02 (reef unchanged at 1.08).
- **Light**: sun +5–8%, sky fill −3–7% in every season, so shadows carry (reef +3% / −4%).
- **Shade colour**: the hemisphere's ground (bounce) colour turned from olive toward blue-green at the
  same brightness and a little less saturated (`0x5f6e3c` → `0x497552` in PNW's spring). The sky colour
  is untouched (0032). Reef's bounce was already teal and stays.
- **Pasture**: map and palette pasture colours a touch richer (saturation ×1.06–1.08) and darker
  (×0.92–0.95), yellow-greens nudged a few degrees toward green, so fields sit in the middle band and
  bare soil and paths read lighter. Reef's sand unchanged.
- Measured spread (5th to 95th percentile of brightness, 0–255, midday): PNW 120 → 126, Atlanta 101 →
  104, Amazon 111 → 115, Sumatra 75 → 82, Serengeti 123 → 124, Chinandega 112 → 115, reef 84 → 89.
  The dark end moved most (shade 4–7 levels darker); the top barely moved.
- `tools/value-capture.mjs`: the before/after camera and value numbers, for the next look sessions.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **How far to push?** This pass is deliberately mild: views read crisper and the shade deeper, but
   the light band (paths, sunlit tops, sand) is still thin: in PNW and Serengeti the top quarter of
   the picture sits within about 15 levels. Going further means contrast ~1.18–1.20, which starts to
   clip sunlit sand and snow on the reef and in winter. *Recommendation:* keep this, and get the light
   band from material colours instead (paths and bare soil a step lighter, sunlit crown tops) as part
   of s3's canopy and soil work, rather than a harder grade.
2. **Shade colour strength.** The bounce light is now clearly green-teal on crown undersides and
   trunks, but flat ground in shade still reads neutral, because flat ground takes its shade light from
   the sky colour, which 0032 says not to blue. *Recommendation:* leave the sky alone; if shade should
   read bluer on open ground, do it with the grade's lift (a tiny blue lift on dark values only) in s4,
   where the night and golden-hour washes move into the lighting anyway.
3. **PNW's spring meadow is now a fresher green** (a bit more saturated than before). *Recommendation:*
   keep; if it reads too bright to you, drop PNW's pasture saturation back to the old values in
   `js/maps/pnw.js` and keep the rest.
4. **Release references.** The release check's pictures will report every map as "changed".
   *Recommendation:* refresh them once at the end of the batch (after s3–s5 also change the look), not
   after each session.

## New ideas noticed

- Sumatra's whole view sits low (median 61 of 255 at midday, Amazon 78): its sunlit crown tops never
  get light. Lighter, yellower top leaves on the emergents (s3's canopy variety) would do more than any
  grade.
- Serengeti's dark-brown soil patches are still the darkest thing on the savanna and darker for the
  extra contrast; s3's laterite pass should lighten them (planned).
- The PNW forest floor under the conifers is mossy olive at the same value as the meadow in places;
  a darker `borderMoss` would separate forest from field at the farm's edge.
