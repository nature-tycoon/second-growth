# Session 6: cougar fitted to real cougars

Cole's request: the cougar should hold up close (the intro film and watch mode show it). Drawing only,
no new shader program, no simulation change.

Pictures in `s6/` (`before` = s5's commit 1704b63, `after` = this commit), from
`tools/animal-closeup-review.html`: `close-*` is the close-up model, `detail-*` the usual one; `side`,
`quarter`, `head`, `headside`, `front`, `walk`, `golden`, `game` (the game's closest zoom).

## How it went

Two attempts shaped by eye failed (Cole: "super fake", then "so much worse"): a face built from extra
parts, then a model fitted to numbers measured off walking, head-low photos, which drooped the neck and
shrank the face. Researching how accurate animal models are made (reference photos matched side on
first; shape models fitted to photos such as SMAL, whose licence is research-only; bought sculpted
models, which Cole found too realistic for the game's look) led to `tools/animal-photo-overlay.html`
(committed earlier, d908464): a real cougar's photo from Wikimedia Commons laid over the model at the
same scale. Then one change at a time, each shown to Cole against the photo before the next.

## Done

- **Overlay page, head mode**: head photos (`head: true`) line up by the nose tip and the near ear's
  front root, scaled and turned, on the close-up model, with the model's outline drawn over the photo.
  Two profile photos added. Whole-animal photos line up by the body (tail base to chest front, and the
  ground), so a head carried high or low doesn't throw it off. A slower earlier photo load no longer
  lands over a newer choice.
- **Cougar body** (the plain-coat cat in `feline`, found by its coat, not its name): legs as long as the
  sprite says (other cats keep 0.85), a shallower chest and haunch; hind thighs reaching back into the
  rump (`thighBack`) with a lower knee (`stifle`) so they taper smoothly; slimmer ankles over broader
  paws (`paw`); twice the rings along each leg (`legSub`). Tail thicker, starting inside the rump below
  the topline, sloping back and down in one line to a dark tip.
- **Head**: a little smaller, lower and further forward (topline flows from back to skull), narrower;
  eyes forward on the face (about halfway from nose to ear root, as in both profile photos), sunk, under
  a brow ridge like the lion's, with a dark tear line from the inner corner; eyes painted on the eyeball
  (pale yellow-amber iris, round pupil, black rim, heavier upper lid); shallower muzzle and jaw; ears
  smaller, lower and wider, turned out, dark-backed; a flat pinkish-brown nose; a rounded white chin with
  a dark lip line that rises to a point under the nose; smaller, lower whisker pads.
- **Measured against three photos**: side-on, the eye at 0.47 of nose-to-ear (photos 0.47-0.50);
  head-on, face width, pad width, eye size and spacing within about 10% of the photo.
- **Face fur** (Cole: "much better"): soft markings in the head's vertex colours (`plainFace`): a darker bridge, a pale spot over each eye and paler cheeks, a white upper lip, dark whisker roots (on the pads too). The eye streaks are short marks from the inner corner. For soft edges the head uses the finer mesh the lion and jaguar use (`detail`).
- **Cost**: triangles, usual model 7.9k -> 19.1k, close-up 19.9k -> 46.4k (one cougar per map; `perf-guard-check` within 10%).
  `?bench=1` filling a 1440x900 frame at 2x: the same as the bobcat and jaguar beside it in each run
  (close-up 1.6-2.0 ms, usual 2.7-3.3 ms, machine busy with checks). Shared program: no per-pixel coat.
- `quadruped()` options, all defaulting to the old look; recorded in 0018's rules.

Checks (checker, before the tear lines were softened, a colour and width change only): `motion-sweep-check pnw` all within limits, the cougar with no foot slide, sinking, wobble or stalls; `animal-culling-check` 17/17; `perf-guard-check` within 10%; `animal-eyes-check` 398 models (rerun after); `species-name-count` below the stored count; `check-release --headless` 154 pass. `first-frame-profile`: PNW 28 programs (unchanged), Sumatra 27, Serengeti first visit 29, saved farm 30. The cougar isn't on Serengeti and its code path adds no program key, so the saved-farm 30 should be that load's usual count; not checked against a clean copy.

The tear lines under the eyes were softened at Cole's word after the pictures he approved (thinner, warm dark brown).

## Open questions for Cole

2. **Other cats.** The bobcat, leopard, cheetah, ocelot and jaguar still have the old cat shape (thick
   tube legs, round faces). *Recommendation:* fit each the same way, two or three photos per species,
   starting with the bobcat (same map as the cougar).
3. **The intro film (s7)** shows a cougar close-up: it now films the new cougar. *Recommendation:* go
   ahead; re-film once and look at the cougar frames before shipping.
