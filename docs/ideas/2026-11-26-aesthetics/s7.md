## Done (biggest mismatch first, each checked against the photos in the overlay)

- **Body and neck**: body shallower (it stood about a fifth too tall at the withers), back level, belly
  tucked; a thick neck leaving the front of the chest at a slant (`neckPath`) instead of a thin upright
  pipe, so the head sits lower and further forward, between the walking doe and the alert buck.
- **Head**: a short skull behind the ears, the ears set at its back (`earFwd`, new); the eye at 0.40 of
  the way from ear root to nose as in the profile photo (was 0.17); a slimmer jaw; the nose a smaller
  sunk dark nose, the tip of the muzzle painted black around it.
- **Ears**: broad leaf ears with the hollow turned out to the side (`earTurn` now works for pointed ears),
  pale inside, dark rim. Cole: "a little too large", so about 12% smaller in both length and width.
- **Eyes**: painted on the eyeball on the close-up (dark brown iris, wide flat pupil, black rim, looking
  out to the side: `deerEye`), set into the head so they don't bulge past its outline; a plain dark eye
  on the usual model (`eyeFar`, new: a painted eye costs about 300 triangles and is a pixel or two there).
- **Face markings** (`deerFace`, vertex colours, finer head rings only: `headSeg`, new): grey-brown face,
  darker forehead and bridge, pale eye ring with a short dark tear-gland mark, white band round the upper
  lip, white chin with a dark spot each side, white patch high on the throat. Cole: head "a little too
  dark", white marks "a little too strong", so the face is lighter and the white marks about a third softer.
- **Ear root and back** (Cole, second look): the ears pinch in to a narrow root (ear shape `deer`); the
  back stays level further toward rump and withers before rounding off (`flatBack`, new, on the body loft).
- **Hind thighs** (Cole: "stick off a little too much, should blend into the rump", then "the back of the
  thigh needs to meet the back of the rump near the tail", a "protruding butt" side on): the thigh's top
  is raised toward the tail (`thighUp`, new) and reaches back to the rump's end, so one line runs from the
  tail down the back of the thigh; its top is narrower side to side (`thighZ`, new) so it stays inside the
  slim rump. Then (Cole: "taper into the rump at the top instead of forming a shelf") the thigh narrows
  toward its top (`thighTaper`, new) so it runs into the rump with no round end. Pulling the thigh forward
  instead (first try) left the rump bulging over it: rejected.
- **Tail**: the white ball is gone; a flat tail hanging from under the rump's top, black on the outside,
  white underneath.
- **Legs**: slimmer cannons, the hind thigh reaching back into the rump with a high stifle (the cougar's
  `thighBack`, `stifle`), and a small pointed hoof instead of a ball. More leg rings and split hooves
  were tried and taken out: +5k triangles on the close-up.
- **Buck**: the same model with the existing forked (mule-type) rack. The dark streaks on the buck's face
  in the review pictures are the antlers' shadows (they're in the before pictures too).
- Overlay page: head-photo mode works for pointed ears (`motion.faceMarks` now has their root).

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Ear insides read dark grey**, not the pale grey-white of the photos: the shared crease shading
   (`bakeSurface`) darkens every cupped ear. *Recommendation:* leave it for now (every mammal's ears do it);
   if it bothers you, a lighter crease floor for ears is one setting for all animals.
2. **White-tailed deer** (Atlanta, Chinandega): same genus and build. *Recommendation:* turn on `slender`
   for them and give them their own face (no dark forehead, white eye ring and nose band stronger, brown
   tail with a white fringe that flags when they run), checked against white-tailed photos the same way.
3. **Elk and sambar** still have the old shared deer shape (thin pipe neck, ball tail on the elk). *Recommendation:* fit the elk next (PNW, beside the deer), with its own photos.
4. **Head carriage**: set between a walking doe and an alert buck. *Recommendation:* keep; lower it a little if the deer look too alert in play.
