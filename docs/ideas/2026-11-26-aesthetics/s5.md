## Done

- **Animal sky rim.** On the side turned from the sun, edges facing the eye and the sky pick up the
  hemisphere light's sky colour (`FAUNA_LIGHT` in `animal-material.js`), weighted toward up-facing edges
  so bellies don't glow. One shared uniform (`skyRim`), set each frame from `SKY_RIM` (6) in scene.js:
  full at play zoom, fading as the view closes in (0.4 at zoom 3.2+), 0.35 at eye level. Reads the
  normalized `geometryNormal` (0018 rule, recorded there). At midday with the sun overhead it's barely
  there; at golden hour the shaded flanks get a faint cool edge (`pnw-golden-animals-closer-*` is the
  clearest). Deliberately faint: at 20 it read as white bellies.
- **Glass menus off on weak GPUs.** `menu-effects-off` now also turns on for any `weakGpu` chip
  (Intel HD/UHD, AMD's integrated Radeon), whatever the Detail level (`ui.js`), and it also drops the
  blur on the watch bar, watch menu, moment card and photo bar. Solid panels (there, on Fast and on
  touch screens) carry a fine paper grain (`--paper-grain`: one 128 px SVG noise tile, drawn once). The
  text-heavy HUD panels (`#toolpanel`, `#infopanel`, `#quest`, `#legend`) are now fully solid there
  instead of 62% see-through with no blur. Recorded in `docs/game-menu-style.md`.
- **Restoration contrast** (ground tints in `terrain.js`, flowers in `flora.js`):
  - native groundcover's tile tint is deeper and more saturated (value x0.93, saturation x1.14; x1.05
    on savanna maps so Serengeti stays gold-green, not lime);
  - invasive, exotic and weedy cover and bare pasture are a little flatter (saturation x0.86): PNW's
    start pasture no longer reads brighter and greener than the restored meadow;
  - bare earth on tiles touching water is darker (up to 20%), damp ground grading into the shore mud.
    Only tiles touching water, because a ground patch repaints one tile beyond what changed;
  - wildflowers bloom in drifts: dots and blossoms per tuft follow the meadow's lush/thin patches
    (`drift`), so colour gathers instead of sprinkling evenly. Flower instances fell about 11% on
    perf-guard's fixtures (blossoms can't exceed one per tuft, so thin patches lose more than lush
    ones gain); baseline refreshed with `--update`. The `after` pictures were taken with a slightly
    lower drift (0.3 + 1.4x) than committed (0.35 + 1.6x).
- `value-capture.mjs` `--animals`, `--scene=start`; `release-fixtures.mjs` scene kind `start`.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Rim strength.** Faint by design; at midday it's hard to see at all. *Recommendation:* keep. If you
   want deer to pop more in grass, raise `SKY_RIM` in scene.js to 9–10 (20 turns bellies white).
2. **Degraded pasture flatter.** The start farm's grass is a little duller than before (it was brighter
   than restored meadow). This touches the first picture and the intro film. *Recommendation:* keep
   (it's the game's promise: the land gets richer); s7 re-films anyway. Undo: `DEGRADED.sat` to 1.
3. **Solid HUD panels on phones.** The text-heavy panels used to be 62% see-through on touch screens;
   now solid paper like the rest. *Recommendation:* keep (text over a moving view reads better, and it
   matches the weak-GPU look); revert the `@media (pointer: coarse)` block's `background-color` line if
   you liked seeing the land through them.
4. **Fewer flowers overall (~11%)**, gathered in drifts. *Recommendation:* keep; if meadows look
   sparser to you in play, raise the drift's floor (0.35 in `flora.js`) rather than the peak.
