# Session 4: Water routes (fish stuck in creeks, hippos and crocodiles in the river)

Cut short by a usage limit: built and partly checked, the final check runs are not confirmed (see Open questions 1).

## Done

- **Routes river fish can swim** (`js/sim/swim-routes.js`, new). A river fish's route only steps from one tile to the next where the water between the two is deep enough the whole way, except for a lip no wider than its 0.35-tile hop. The route-finder (`pathTo`), the fish's own target choice, and escaping a failed catch (`evadeHunt`, `fleeAlong`) all use this rule. Results are cached per game day and per depth. A player's height edit clears the cache (`terrainEdit` calls `forgetSwimRoutes`). Fish leaving the map and reef fish are left out.
- **A fish whose way is cut turns away.** When shallow water blocks a fish (`a.swimHeld`, set in `riverFishStep`) for 3 seconds, `recoverMovement` shuts that tile-to-tile step for the rest of the day, and the fish finds a new route or other water. A fish only queued behind another never shuts a step (from the regression review).
- **Every river fish stops only in a tile deep enough at its centre.** Before, only the catfish did. Otherwise every fish aimed at the same sliver of water at the edge of a mostly dry marsh tile. When no deep water is reachable that day, a fish that has no `fishDepth` trait stops where it can (`a.noDeep`), instead of running a 4000-tile search every half second. A fish moved aside by spacing still rests in swimmable water.
- **Hippos and crocodiles.** This was not the dry-strip bug. In the river, a walker braked to 15% pace behind a herd mate resting there, because the session-3 rule ("edge round a standing animal at 0.65 pace") only covered dry ground. It now covers water too (`spacing.steer`).
- **Motion sweep:** removed the KNOWN entries for "river swimmers frozen mid-route" and "semi-aquatic walkers held up in the river". On the first version of the fix, the sweep showed river-fish stall and hippo/croc crawl within limits without them.
- **Pile probe** (per 1000 fish samples, before → first version of the fix):
  - Sumatra 91: 584 → 13
  - Chinandega 91: 292 → 93
  - Sumatra 7: 33 → 2
  - Chinandega 1987: 209 → 9
  - Sumatra 1987, Chinandega 7, Amazon 91 and PNW 91 (147, 0, 19 and 0 after) have no before numbers yet.
- **Checks:** the `checker` ran 19 headless checks on the first version and all passed, including fish-water, fish-swim, salmon-bear, water, beaver-pond, hollis-water, positioning, steering, fishing, reef and species-name-count.
