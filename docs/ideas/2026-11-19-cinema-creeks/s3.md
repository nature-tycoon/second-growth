# Session 3: Movement bugs (clumping, slow deer)

## Done

- **Slow deer, cause 1 (came back on 2026-11-01).** A deer or elk family leader slowed to a quarter pace whenever any member lagging 3 tiles behind was standing still. Grown herd members have no place in the family's formation, so nothing ever woke them to catch up, and the leader crept along for seconds at a time. Now she only all-but-waits for her own calf (which does get woken). Other laggers get the gentler 0.7 / 0.5 slow-down from before. She now slows for her calf on any walk, from 2.5 tiles back, not only on long trips: without the old crawl, a calf fell 3.7 tiles behind on a short walk after a journey (the cervid check allows 3.5; it's 2.83 now, 3.42 before). `rangePaces`, `js/sim/animal-trails.js`.
- **Slow deer, cause 2, and the bunching.** A walking animal braked to 15% pace behind any animal of its own layer standing in its lane. One that's grazing never moves off, so followers crawled behind it for up to 12 seconds, queued nose to tail. That's the "pile" look. Now a walker never brakes for one standing still on the ground: it eases to 0.65 pace while that animal is in its lane and steps round it. It still brakes behind one that's walking (single file). (Easing only near contact let followers press into resting buffalo herds, so it eases the whole way past.) `spacing.steer`, `js/sim/animal-positioning.js`.
- Result (headless probe, three maps × two seeds, 12 game days): deer slowed under 45% pace for more than 2 seconds went from up to 12 s at a time to none longer than 2.1 s.
- **Motion sweep guards** (`tools/motion-sweep-check.mjs`), with per-species rates and limits like the others:
  - `crawl`: a ground walker walking under 35% of its own walking pace (allowing for trotting to keep up, wading, river crossings) for 2 seconds.
  - `clump`: a ground walker or river fish with 2 or more of its own kind within 1.5 body lengths for 4 seconds. A mother and young, littermates, rivals in a bout and a pack on a kill don't count.
  - On the code before this session, 13 limits break: crawl for deer, elk, zebra, gazelle, impala, giraffe, elephant, buffalo and cattle, and clump for deer and buffalo. After the fix, all pass.
- Two older glitches the new counts turned up are listed as known in the sweep, not hidden: hippos and crocodiles stuck mid-route in the river (same rate before this change), and arapaima swimming sideways (3.1 per 1000 before, 5.0 now, only because the random stream shifted).
- Balance (seeded `calibrate`, 15 years): PNW, 5 seeds, score 66 (60–66) before, 63 (56–65) after. Native .45 → .41, herbivores 87 → 82 kg/ha. Atlanta, 3 seeds, 34 → 36. The ranges overlap. Any movement change reshuffles every random draw after it, so I read this as noise, not a shift; see open question 4.
- Decisions 0003 and 0025 updated.
- `tools/river-fish-pile-probe.mjs` (a probe, not a check) measures river fish piling up in a game grown for three years. See open question 1.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **River fish pile up at lips they can't cross. Fix it next?** In games grown for a few years, 20–30% of snakehead and snapper samples are in piles of 4 or more on some seeds. The cause: a fish's route between two pools crosses a strip of ground standing above the water that's wider than the 0.35-tile hop it's allowed (on Sumatra, a marsh tile with its whole north half dry). It presses at the edge for days and the ones behind stack up. I tried the quick fix (every fish, not just the catfish, only picks stopping places deep enough). It moved the piles about without shrinking them, so I reverted it. The real fix is routes that only cross water a fish can swim, which touches the shared route-finder and the salmon runs. *Recommend: yes, as its own session.* It's the same bug as the sweep's long-known "river swimmers frozen mid-route".
2. **Hippos and crocodiles get stuck mid-route in the river** (the new `crawl` count found it; it's older than this change). *Recommend: fold it into the fish routing session above.* It's likely the same dry-strip problem for semi-aquatic walkers.
3. **Walking past a grazing animal at 0.65 pace instead of waiting behind it.** Walkers going through a grazing herd now ease to 0.65 the whole way past, where before they walked at 0.7–1 and then stopped dead behind anyone in their lane. *Recommend: keep it.* Real deer step round a grazing herd mate rather than queueing behind it, and the sweep's glide, trunk and wet counts stay within limits.

4. **PNW's median score dipped 3 points (66 → 63) in the 5-seed balance run**, within the seed range. *Recommend: accept it.* If the next PNW calibrate still sits low, look at whether deer families now graze more spread out.

## New ideas noticed

- The motion sweep only watches about 8 seconds per map and seed, and it starts with whole herds spawned onto a few tiles. A slow pile-up that builds over minutes needs a grown game (as in the fish probe). A once-a-week longer sweep on a grown game would catch slow drifts like this one.
- Herd members without a formation slot are never pulled back to their family by `cervidNeedsRejoin`. They wander back on their own, but a family can stay strung out longer than it should.
- Catfish crowding into the last deep pool in Serengeti's dry season is real (African catfish do this). It would make a nice "moment" for cinema mode.
