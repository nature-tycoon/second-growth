# Session 2: Cinema mode & touch camera

## Done

- **Time of year in cinema mode.** Small and faint in the top letterbox bar, right side: the map's own season name, then the date as the top bar writes it ("Spring · March 7, Year 2"; "Spring · Mar 7 · Y2" under 960 px wide, so on phones). Fades in with the bars, hides during a keystone moment. Spanish map translates both parts.
- **Click or tap an animal to lock on.** In the usual view and at eye level. The director stays with that animal with no time limit (shuffle can't move it on). A small chip at the top left says "Locked on Roosevelt elk ✕". Let go by clicking empty ground, Esc, the chip's ✕, or Next. Letting go doesn't cut away; the director moves on about 5 s later. Esc lets go first, then a second Esc leaves cinema mode as before.
- Picking an animal at eye level now looks through the eye camera. Before, it used the overhead camera, so a click at eye level would have picked the wrong animal or none.
- **Two-finger twist turns the map on phones and tablets**, round the point between your fingers, alongside the existing pan and pinch. A twist only counts after about 11°, so the small wobble in a normal pinch never turns the map.
- New check `tools/watch-lock-browser-check.mjs` (date, lock/release by click, Esc, ✕, eye level, twist direction, pinch wobble). Decision 0020 updated.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Should a keystone moment keep your lock?** Right now a moment (a hunt cinematic, say) takes the camera and drops the lock, and afterwards the director picks freely again. *Recommend: leave it.* Moments are rare and the best shots. Going back to your locked animal afterwards is a small add if you miss it.
2. **Should two-finger twist also work inside cinema mode?** Right now it only turns the map in the normal game. In cinema mode, two fingers do nothing and a drag at eye level already swings round the animal. *Recommend: leave it.* The director moves the camera there, and a twist would fight its slow drift.
3. **Esc now takes two presses to leave cinema mode while locked** (first lets go, second leaves). *Recommend: keep it.* It matches how Esc closes the species list first.
