## Done

- **Winter ice on the fall** (drawing only, nothing saved). The game has no temperature, so the snowpack is the cold signal (`game.snow`: it builds on snowy days, lingers through winter sun, washes off in rain or spring). After about two snowy days settle, the fall starts to freeze. By four it is fully iced. It thaws as the snow melts and eases in and out over a few seconds on screen.
  - The falling sheet's two sides freeze into a blue-white rim. Columns of ice of different lengths hang down from it, like icicles off the lip. The middle always keeps running, with at most 0.3 of the width frozen on each side.
  - Shelf ice forms round the plunge pool's rim, ragged on its inner edge. The churn where the water lands stays open.
  - The rock ledge glazes with ice where the spray wets it (`rockIce`). Its dry tops take snow like the ground around them, which was an idea noticed in session 5.
  - The same freeze reaches a beaver dam's spill, which is also falling water.
- **Waterfall sound.** The fall is now heard close by: full within a tile or two of where the camera looks, fading out by 8 tiles. It is silent from the default overhead view. It fades in as you zoom in and is full at eye level. It plays louder than the creek's murmur beside it, and its level changes smoothly like the other ambience layers. It is a new `falls` layer in the ambience (`sampleSoundLand` → `natureMix.falls`), with no new audio system.
  - **It plays the NPS stream recording for now** (public domain, already in the game), not a waterfall recording. See question 1.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **May I download a real waterfall recording?** Our rule (0002) is to ask you before downloading any sound. Candidates on Freesound:
   - innov8ting's small cascade above a waterfall in Wales: CC0, https://freesound.org/people/innov8ting/sounds/627938/
   - InspectorJ's "Waterfall, Small" series: likely CC BY 4.0, so the licence needs checking, https://freesound.org/people/InspectorJ/sounds/365921/
   - NPS recordings: public domain, but no waterfall page turned up in their sound gallery.

   Freesound needs a sign-in to download, so you may need to grab the file yourself (a few MB as WAV). I'd trim it to about 20 s and encode it small like the others. Until then the fall plays the stream recording, louder.
   *Recommend: the CC0 one (innov8ting). Once the file is in, swapping it in is one line (`RECORDED_BEDS.falls`) plus the credits.*
2. **The ice is fairly subtle.** Snowy weather dims and greys the whole scene, so the blue-white rims read softly, and the glaze on the rock is patchy rather than streaked.
   *Recommend: keep it subtle. Real falls only ice at the edges. If you want it bolder, I can widen the rims or brighten the ice.*
3. **Ice follows snow lying on the ground, not air temperature.** A cold, dry, clear spell with no snow won't ice the fall, because the game doesn't model temperature.
   *Recommend: keep it. It ties ice to what you can see (snow on the valley) and adds no new simulation.*

## New ideas noticed

- The pond and creek water doesn't freeze at all in a snowy winter. Thin edge ice on still ponds would match the fall's ice, using the same snowpack signal and drawing only.
- With the fall icing over, session 7's American dipper is a good fit: dippers keep diving in open winter water right beside the ice.
- The creek and river ambience doesn't change with zoom (it's the same from far overhead), while the fall now does. Fading all water sounds with zoom might make the overhead view calmer.
