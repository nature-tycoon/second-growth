# Session 5: Waterfall look

Before/after pictures: [s5-shots/](s5-shots/) (`before-*` and `after-*`: eye level from the side, overhead, the dam spill).

## Done

- **A rock ledge** (`ledge` in `js/render3d/terrain.js`): the fall now pours off a slab of faceted, flat-shaded stone. Its top sits just under the water for the first 40% of the tile. Its face curves down behind the falling water. Wings reach a little way into the banks on each side, mossy on top and dark and wet near the water. The map's height grid can't hold a sheer cliff (the ground under a fall is a steep ramp), so the rock is drawn standing on that ramp. The wings sink into the bank toward their ends. However the bank is raised or lowered, they stand at most a low outcrop (0.06) over it.
- **Falling water** (`fallMesh`, with the new `fallLook` material in `js/render3d/terrain-materials.js`): the water runs level over the ledge, then falls on an arc. It starts thick, dark and glassy at the lip. As it falls it thins and tears into white streaks racing down, brighter where it's thin. The streaks scroll in the shader; nothing moves on the processor.
- **Foam and mist**: a foam disc boils outward in broken rings on the pool where the water lands. Four soft mist puffs hang over the pool and drift. The mist fades out once a tile is small on screen, so the normal overhead view draws none and it shows when you zoom in or watch at eye level. The mist is subtle, white over white water.
- **Beaver dam spill (session 4), seen and fixed**: it was a clear, hard-edged glass card in front of the dam. It is now the same falling water, thinner, with soft sides and a little foam, and no rock or mist. I widened it from 0.6 to 0.85 of each mound. Its foam sat just under a pond's drawn surface, so it now sits on the drawn water.
- **Cost**: one more mesh (the rock), PNW only. play-profile on PNW (High, 3 years), before → after: GPU 9.0–9.9 → 9.4–10.2 ms; drawing on the processor 1.8–6.8 → 1.7–7.0 ms; simulation unchanged. Everything stays within the ceilings (GPU 11, drawing 8, sim 3), and the moves are within run-to-run noise. perf-guard passes and its baseline is unchanged.
- **Checks**: `waterfall-check` now checks the rock ledge (it sits under the water's top, stays small, and never stands out of the bank), the sheet's facing, the foam and the mist. `beaver-dam-growth-check` checks that the spill is thinner than a fall and has no mist. All 12 checks from `impact.mjs --run` pass. `regression-reviewer` ran on the diff; I fixed its findings: the mist faded by window height instead of screen size, the rock could stand out of a lowered bank, and two records were stale.
- Decision records 0050 and 0034 and the AGENTS.md line are updated.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **The fall is still only about 5 m tall and slopes forward a little.** A taller, sheer fall would need a finer height grid or the land itself reshaped, which is far bigger work.
   *Recommend: keep this size. It reads as a ledge fall now; see the after pictures.*
2. **The mist is faint and shows only up close.** I kept it light so it doesn't wash the fall out, and the normal overhead view draws none of it.
   *Recommend: keep it. If you'd like it stronger, it's one number (`0.38` in `fallLook`).*
3. **The test dam spills over only part of its line.** It spills only where there's water below a mound, and at the test site the dam's other mounds sit on the bank at a bend. That's how the simulation places it, not a drawing fault.
   *Recommend: leave it; real dams spill unevenly too.*
