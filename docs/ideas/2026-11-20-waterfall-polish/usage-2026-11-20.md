# Usage review, 2026-11-20

Last 7 days (**B** = Nov 14–20, UTC) against the three weeks before (**A** = Oct 24–Nov 13).
PostHog project 630638, read-only SQL. Aggregate numbers only. Cole's own play is in these numbers
and can't be separated out (no tag for it), so on a base this small it can shift the totals by a few players.

## In short

- **More people are playing.** B had about 12 players a day, against 8–9 a day in A. The week had 71 players and 103 play sessions, against an average of 51 players and 68 sessions a week in A. This is the best week so far.
- **The growth is mostly itch.io.** itch brought 24 players in B, against 15 in all of A. The website brings fewer than in its launch weeks (39 in B, 100 in A).
- **Players don't come back.** Only about 1 new player in 12 plays again within a week. That rate was the same before and after the recent changes. This is the biggest problem.
- **The new Free Play start works at its first step but loses people right after.** More new players finish a goal (66%, was 48%). But more of them now leave inside 2 minutes (45%, was 37%), and fewer stay 15 minutes or longer (21%, was 36%).
- **Frame rate got slightly worse on desktop**, and a third of desktop players now run under 20 fps. Loading takes about 8 seconds for a first visit.
- Most of what shipped Nov 14–19 (new loading screen, water fixes, wildlife batch, the Nov 19 lag and UI fixes) is **too new to measure**, and the cinema-creeks work isn't live yet.

**Devices.** In B, 52 players started on desktop, 16 on phones and 3 on tablets. Phones lose more players at every step: 16 start, 12 use a tool and 6 reach year 1. On desktop the same steps are 52, 40 and 28.

## Where players drop off (new players, Nov 13–20)

1. **The first 2 minutes.** 33 of 73 new players leave this early. 13 of those 33 are desktop players arriving from itch.io: 13 of the 22 new itch desktop players leave inside 2 minutes, against 11 of 30 on the website. 9 of the 33 are on phones, and 8 of those 9 saw the "turn your phone" prompt. About half of the quick leavers never use a tool.
2. **Minutes 5 to 15.** Last week 62 players left a game. 13 of them hit a "not enough money" refusal, 18 were told "unsuitable ground" and 32 lost a species during the session. These counts only started on Nov 12, so there's nothing to compare them with yet.
3. **The next day.** Fewer than 1 player in 10 comes back. No change has moved this yet.

## Fix next (ranked)

1. **Give players a reason to come back tomorrow.** Evidence: only 4–5 new players per week return within 7 days, in every week (~8%), and nothing shipped has changed that. Ideas: a "while you were away" summary of what grew and arrived, a visible next goal at exit, and the newsletter prompt (shown once in 28 days).
2. **The itch.io first 2 minutes.** Evidence: itch is now the main source of growth (24 players in B against 15 in all of A), but 13 of its 22 new desktop players leave inside 2 minutes and about half never use a tool. Look at what the itch embed shows first: the frame size, the click to start, the sound and the first prompt. Watch a few short itch sessions in replay.
3. **Desktop frame rate on weak laptops.** Evidence: 34% of desktop players ran under 20 fps in B, against 19% in A. Their slow frames rose from 16% to 21%. In B, 17 desktop players were moved to Fast at start because of their graphics chip. The old-forest lag fix (Nov 19) may help; re-check this next week before starting new work. Use `node tools/play-profile.mjs`.
4. **Upright phones.** Evidence: 8 of the 9 phone players who left inside 2 minutes saw the rotate prompt. Phones also have the lowest share reaching year 1 (6 of 16). Either let the opening play upright, or make the prompt friendlier (show what the game looks like turned).
5. **The step after the first goal.** Evidence: more new players finish a first goal (66%), but fewer stay 15 minutes (21%, was 36%), and only 8% take the Story offer. After the first goal pays, point players to a second, bigger goal. Also check that the money and "unsuitable ground" refusals explain what to do instead (13 and 18 of 62 leavers hit them).
6. **First-visit loading (~8 s median, 18 s for the slowest tenth).** This is lower priority, because nobody is recorded leaving during loading. `loading_left` didn't fire at all in 28 days, so first confirm that the event still works before trusting that.
