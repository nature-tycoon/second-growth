2. **Should new players start in Free Play now that Story mode is rarely chosen?** Campaign starts fell from 64 to 6, and the Story offer is taken 8% of the time. Recommended: keep Free Play first, because more players finish a first goal. Make the second goal bigger, and offer Story after it instead of early.
3. **Should we look at the itch.io page and embed?** Recommended: yes. Watch 5 short itch sessions in replay and check what shows in the first 30 seconds. itch is where new players come from now, and 13 of its 22 new desktop players left inside 2 minutes.
4. **Can your own play be tagged so it stays out of these numbers?** Recommended: yes, with a one-time `?internal=1` that sets a PostHog person property. With about 12 players a day, a few of your sessions skew the totals.
