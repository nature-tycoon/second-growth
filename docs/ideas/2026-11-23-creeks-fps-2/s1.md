# Session 1: UI follow-ups

## Done
- **Creek tool hint.** When a creek stroke ends short of the cursor because the turn was too tight (0054), the tool flags it (`stroke.stoppedShort`, `ToolActions.end`) and the UI shows once per device: "Creeks bend gently: swing the stroke wider." (`creekBendHint`, key `sg-creek-bend-hint`). Covered in `creek-cutoff-check` (straight stroke: no flag; a stroke that doubles back: flag).
- **Flood cut-off note and marker.** `Events.finishFlood` adds a journal-only line ("A flood cut a new channel across a tight bend of the creek. The old loop is marsh now.") pointing at the middle of the new channel, and emits `creek-cut` with that spot. The minimap draws a breathing ring there for 2 minutes. No cinematic, no pop-up. The flood's own notice is unchanged. Covered in `creek-cutoff-check` (one event, one journal line, both at the cut).
- **Flood-risk overlay** now recolours within 0.1 s of a ground edit instead of up to 0.8 s (`Renderer.updateOverlay`, follows `world.hv`). Measured: `floodRisk()` costs about 0.5 ms (PNW) and 0.3 ms (Sumatra), plus one overlay texture upload, and only while that overlay is on and the ground is being edited, at most 10 times a second.
- **Contact page.** The ☰ menu's "Send feedback" is now "Contact" (and the "Get updates by email" item under More is gone). The page holds the feedback form and, below it, the email signup (`updatesInto` in `ui/newsletter.js`; hidden while no backend). Backend, endpoints and error codes untouched; `backend-check` passes. Spanish: "Contacto". The ☰-menu button hook `feedback` and `#btn-feedback` stay.
- **Lens regression check** in `tools/lens-depth-browser-check.mjs`: a selected great horned owl in `feed` with a rabbit as `a.prey` gives `fauna.tracked.length === 2` and `pose.meal.r > 0`; unselected it tracks nothing. Passes.
