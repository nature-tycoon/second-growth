## Done

- **Floods follow every creek.** The flooded-land map (the flood itself, its plant losses and silt,
  and the Flood risk overlay) now spreads from the river and from every creek: above a culvert or
  below, dug or the map's own. A culvert passes water; it still stops fish, and fish passage
  (`w.connected`) is unchanged. Height limits, the 3-tile creek reach, buildings, dams and roads work
  as before.
- **Changed from the brief, and why:** with more creeks walking out, the old single walk would have let
  a creek take a tile the river would have flooded (a creek's limit is lower), so some river floodplain
  could have stopped flooding (the plan review found 1 such tile on Amazon, and more beside a dug creek).
  The river and the creeks are now walked separately and the results joined. Measured on fresh
  maps: no tile that flooded before stops flooding, on any map, at normal or big strength.
- **How much more floods** (fresh maps, dry tiles at strength 1.0 / 1.8, before → after): PNW 568/1033
  → 660/1307, Amazon 128/1006 → 185/1184, Chinandega 89/326 → 121/429, Sumatra 411/1632 → 518/1845,
  Serengeti +6/+10, Atlanta +2/+2, reef none. On PNW a big flood now covers 117 tiles beside the
  ditch above the culvert (none before). Sumatra's gain is mostly land beside its canals that
  the river's walk had claimed and refused.
- **Plant and tree losses along the upper ditch** (Hollis restore plan, 8 years, seeds 7 and 11):
  trees within 3 tiles of the upper ditch 14 → 6 and 10 → 3; shrubs 276 → 274 and 320 → 271. The trees
  lost are young birch and bigleaf maple (not flood-tolerant); alder, cottonwood, ash and willow
  stand. That matches a real floodplain. Players who follow the opening already pull the culvert,
  which made the upper ditch flood before this change too.
- **Flood cut-offs (0054)** work with the wider flood: their own height gate already matched the
  creek rule, so no logic changed. A cut on a creek dug off the upper ditch still happens, and its new
  channel carries no flood mark.
- **Checks:** new `flood-reach-check` (4: upper ditch floods and fish passage unchanged; every map
  floods only within the height rule, never water or buildings; a creek dug beside the river takes
  no river floodplain; cut-off on the upper ditch), `creek-cutoff-check` (9), `water-shape-check`
  (57), `impact --run`, `water-check`, `hollis-water-check`, `species-name-count`, `perf-guard-check`
  all pass. Flood risk overlay: 0.14 → 0.20 ms (PNW).
- **Calibrate all 15 3** (before: unmodified code; after: this change): scores PNW 77 → 75, Atlanta
  36 → 35, Serengeti 72 → 71, Amazon 42 → 45, Sumatra 45 → 43, Chinandega 49 → 47, reef 90 → 90.
  More flooded tiles draw more random numbers as they recede, so every seeded run's weather and
  wildlife diverge; the species swings (rabbit, sloth, monitor, boa) read as seed noise. PNW's water
  sign −0.06 is the one shift that's plausibly this change.
- Reviews: `regression-reviewer` on plan (walk ownership, opening plantings, notice wording) and diff
  (no blockers; the record now names `js/sim/events.js` so `impact.mjs` finds 0034).

## Open questions for Cole

1. **A player-dug creek with no outlet floods its banks too.** All creeks count, even a ditch dug on
   its own in a field. Recommended: keep (any channel carries storm water, and the 3-tile, low-land
   rule keeps it small). The other way would be to count only creeks that reach the river, culverts
   allowed.
2. **The flood notice still says "the river spilled over N tiles".** Now some of those tiles are
   along creeks. Recommended: leave it (the Spanish already says "the water"); change it only if a
   player reads it as wrong.
3. **Young birch and maple along the upper ditch now die in floods.** Recommended: keep. That's real,
   and the streamside mix's willow and dogwood and the pioneer mix's alder and cottonwood stand.

## New ideas noticed

- The Flood risk overlay now shows the upper ditch's banks. A one-line tip on the overlay ("creeks
  above a culvert flood too; culverts only stop fish") could make that clear.
- Calibrate's three seeds can't tell a small ecology shift from noise once a change moves the random
  stream. A `calibrate` mode that holds the random stream steady (separate random streams per
  system) would make before/after runs cleaner.
