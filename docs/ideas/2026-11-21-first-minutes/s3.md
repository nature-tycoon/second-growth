## Open questions for Cole
1. **One button tears out the whole north and east fence (200 tiles), not just the stretch by the deer.** My recommendation: keep it. It's the "Wildlife corridors" goal in one satisfying click, it costs $500 net after the goal reward, and a partial gap would only bring in the one group. The prompt's button teaches less about the Demolish tool itself. Later sessions can mention that Demolish does the same by hand.
2. **The waiting deer stand still in daylight for as long as the prompt is up (up to ~90 game days).** Real deer at a forest edge mill about and browse, and they're most active at dawn and dusk. My recommendation: fine for now. The prompt is usually answered in under a minute. If playtests show players leaving them standing, give them a slow browse along the edge.
3. **`farm_opened` is also the name share.js already sends when someone opens a shared farm** (`{ map, from }`), so the two mix in PostHog. My recommendation: rename the share one to `shared_farm_opened` in a small follow-up. I kept the name the plan reserved.
4. **Everywhere, not just on a first visit, fence-blocked arrivals now wait at the edge and may come in later through a gap.** My recommendation: keep it. It's ecologically neutral while they wait, and it rewards partial fence removal. Seeded calibrate on all seven maps: every score within 1 point, and no fenced herbivore moved.
5. **Amazon and Serengeti skip the fence step.** Tapirs' land can't hold them yet, and Serengeti's herds come by the river crossing. They get the culvert prompt only. Sumatra gets it with sambar. My recommendation: keep it. Showing an animal at the fence that the land can't hold would be untrue.
