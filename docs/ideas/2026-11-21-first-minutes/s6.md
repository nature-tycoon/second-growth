## Open questions for Cole
1. **The story text still says "second-growth forest to the north and east".** The forest is now drawn as big, mature conifers. Mature second growth (80–100 years) looks like this, so the text is still true. My recommendation: leave it. "Old forest" in the plan means how it looks, and the farm story reads better with logged-over neighbours.
2. **At eye level, the border floor is now a mossy turf texture rather than duff** (reviewer note, decision 0019). Under real PNW conifers the floor is mostly moss, ferns and needles, so I think it's right. My recommendation: keep it, and look once in watch mode at the fence.
3. **The start view costs 10 more draw calls and about 0.4 ms more GPU** (table above). My recommendation: keep it. If session 7's first-10-seconds work shows draw calls are the bottleneck on weak laptops, drop the two rarest border shrubs.
4. **Other maps' borders are unchanged** (Atlanta's and Sumatra's forest sides could use the same edge treatment). My recommendation: leave them until their first-visit views matter. 64 of 71 new players start on PNW.
