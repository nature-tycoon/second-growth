# Intro film: shot list (for Cole to approve)

**See it with the map:** `http://localhost:8347/tools/film-shots.html` (with `python3 tools/serve.py` running), or from the review page's "Shot list and map" link. Click a shot to light its camera on the map; drag the time bar to see where the animals are at each moment.

About 33 seconds. Everything happens in one place you already know: where the creek runs out of the old forest, down over the north fence and the waterfall. All the action is within 45 m of the fence. 1 tile = 8 m.

**The style:** few shots, held long. The camera stands still or moves slowly, like a wildlife film crew on a tripod. Close shots use a long lens from 30–40 m away, so the animals never know we're there. Animals move at real speed (the doe's gallop 12 m/s, the cougar's sprint 15 m/s for under 3 s, its stalk under 1 m/s), and the elk at the end walk.

**Where things are:** the creek leaves a strip of salmonberry through the forest with a line of alders on each side (bare in March) and no big trees. That gap is the "sunlit gap". It's also why every camera here can see its animal: the gap gives clear lines of sight down it and across it.

## The chase, beat by beat (real seconds; film seconds differ only in the slow motion)

- 0–15.9: the doe browses at the gap's east side, facing east-south-east. The cougar lies 13 m west of her, slightly behind her.
- 9.8–15.9: the cougar creeps 4 m toward her (0.5–0.9 m/s), freezing once.
- 16.0: the doe's head snaps up. The cougar freezes, 9 m from her.
- 17.0: both go. She springs south down the gap, away from it. It rushes.
- 17.84–18.39: the pounce: a 9 m leap that lands behind her (film 17.9–19.0, in slow motion).
- 18.4–20.4: she gallops on; the cougar runs on 25 m more, slows, and stops at the last trees (film 21.2). Its whole sprint is under 3 s and about 35 m. It is never ahead of her: its closest is 2 m behind.
- 20.97–21.66: she clears the fence (film 21.7–22.4) and runs down the field past the falls, slowing to bounds.

## Checked (numbers in [f2.md](f2.md))

Every camera has a clear line to its animals' heads for the whole shot, through the real trunks, logs, bushes and ground of the start game. Every animal stays within 6 tiles of the farm, under the 9-tile limit. Speeds are within real limits. What the check can't see: ferns, low conifer branches and leaves don't block sight lines. In F3, the motion check on the staged frames will test that again with the real poses.
