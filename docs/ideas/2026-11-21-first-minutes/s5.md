# Session 5/10: Visible animals at the start

## Done
- **Why the farm looked empty.** A fresh PNW farm had plenty of hunting ground for a hawk and a coyote, but the game counted their food before the starting voles were placed (so their room was 0), and only 6 voles were seeded. Hawks and coyotes therefore turned up only in year 1–2.
- **Starting voles counted.** The starting animals are now placed before the game works out each species' room (`js/game.js`), so a hunter's room counts the prey already there. This applies to all maps; calibrate showed no change on the other six.
- **A red-tailed hawk from day 1.** It starts over the field just south of the north fence, where the opening's camera looks, and it hunts the voles. In the first minute it is on the wing 45–88% of the time.
- **More voles.** The PNW start now has 14 Townsend's voles instead of 6. Worn-out pasture is real vole country, the land has room for 34, and with 14 the hawk's room is about 1.75, so the starting state holds together.
- **American crows, a new PNW species** (`crow`, *Corvus brachyrhynchos*). Five start feeding on the field below the forest edge and settle at about 10–14. They live in farm fields and meadows near trees. They are drawn as a black songbird with black legs and a heavier bill (two new optional sprite fields, `beak` and `legs`). They walk rather than hop, and they don't flee hawks. Nothing eats them, and they eat nothing that is simulated. They are added last in the species list, so old saves keep their species numbers. The field-guide portrait was rendered by the game, and the homepage now says 411 species.
- **A coyote passing through.** New trait `ranges`, set on the PNW coyote only. While no coyotes live on the farm and it has prey now, a coyote comes in from its side about once every 40 days on Standard. It hunts for 5–10 days, then leaves (`a.passUntil`, saved). A passing coyote holds no home and doesn't breed, and the month's arrival odds don't count it as a resident. None come during a first visit's fence and culvert prompts. With more voles, coyotes now settle within a few months instead of years. Rule added to decision 0013.
- **What players see.** In headless Chrome the opening's fence view shows the hawk and at least two crows for the whole first minute, with the deer walking in (screenshot checked). `first-minutes-browser-check` now asserts this at 812×375 and 1280×800, and both pass.

## Open questions for Cole
1. **The crows have no call yet.** Decision 0002 says a new sound must be a real field recording, downloaded only with your OK. My recommendation: yes, add one CC0 American crow call (Xeno-canto or Wikimedia, about 50 KB). Crows are the loudest thing on a real farm field.
2. **The ecosystem score starts a little higher** (about +2 points: the hawk and crows count as wildlife species from day 1), and the 15-year median rose from 70 to 76, partly because coyotes and hawks settle sooner. My recommendation: keep it. These animals really live there, and a few hundred dollars more in early grants doesn't loosen the $30k start much. If you'd rather crows didn't count toward health, since they're generalists that thrive on degraded land, we could mark them as not counting. I'd leave them in for now.
3. **Coyotes now settle in year 1 instead of years 1–2,** because the start has more voles. My recommendation: keep it. A coyote on old pasture is the most ordinary sight in western Washington, and the rare animals (cougar, salmon, beavers) are still earned.
4. **One new trait, `ranges`, is set only on the PNW coyote.** Jackals on the Serengeti, or a fox in Atlanta, would act the same way. My recommendation: leave the other maps for now, and add it there only with a calibrate run per map.
