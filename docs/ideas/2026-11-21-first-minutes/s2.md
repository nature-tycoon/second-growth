# Session 2/10: Quiet first minutes

## Done
- **Quiet opening minutes, first visit only** (`js/ui/ui.js` `holdInFirstMinutes`, `QUIET_MS`). For 5 real minutes after a first visit's start, notices the simulation raises on its own (`Game.notify` now tags them `sim: true` while `Game.update` runs) don't pop up if they're alarms (`warn`, `fire`, `flood`, `bad`), and other news (`good`, `discover`, `info`, `season`) pops up at most once every 45 s. Everything still goes in the Field journal. Fire and flood banners and the "pause on events" setting stay quiet in that window too. Not held: replies to the player (refusals, not enough money, undo), goals, tips, anything with a button, and the fence warning (tagged `keep: true` in `wildlife-population.js`, since session 3 makes it the opening's hook). Returning players are unchanged.
- **Story offer waits for a lull** (`firstGoalNote`, `maybeOfferStory`). The first goal now only queues the offer (`storyPending`). It shows when the player pauses, goes 25 s without a click, key or scroll, or opens the menu (where the Story button reads "Want a guided start? Story mode, on this farm"). It never shows on a goal or in the middle of a stroke. `story_offered` now carries `from: pause | idle | menu`.
- **Taking the offer no longer starts a new farm** (`startStoryHere`). It used to open the mode chooser, which (with the autosave already there) asked twice and then ran `newGame` (`game_restart`). That's the "reload" player 12 saw. It wasn't a page reload: a page reload only happens on a map or language switch. Now Chapter 1 opens on the same farm: same land, same day, same money, no `game_restart` or `map_switch`. The menu's own "Story mode" button still opens the chooser unless the offer is waiting.
- **Refused buildings say where they fit** (`js/tools.js` `buildSpot`, `nearestBuildSpot`). "Not enough clear, dry ground…" and "…has to sit right beside a road…" now add "It fits about N tiles to the north-east: click this note to look there." The note is clickable and centres the view on the spot. If nothing fits, it says to lay a Gravel road. This applies everywhere, not just the first minutes, because the refusal was a dead end at any time. Placement rules are unchanged. The search only proposes spots fully inside the property. Planting refusals already named a better mix (`refusal` / `suitedTool`), so they didn't need a change.
- Check: `tools/first-minutes-browser-check.mjs` extended (held alarms, good news one at a time, fence and replies still shown, journal keeps all, refused center says where, no offer while busy, offer on pause (phone size) and idle (desktop size), taking it starts Chapter 1 on the same farm). Both sizes pass, and so does play-analytics-browser-check. Headless passes: tool-actions, sandbox, sim-rng, species-name-count.

## Open questions for Cole
1. **5 minutes of quiet, or end it early once the player has used several tools?** My recommendation: a flat 5 minutes. The noisiest trail (player 11: ~30 notices, floods, a wildfire) came from the busiest player, so ending early on tool use would have quieted no one who needed it.
2. **Should the fence warning be quiet too?** I left it showing because session 3 turns it into the opening moment. My recommendation: session 3 decides, and if its moment replaces the warning, drop the `keep` tag.
3. **Taking Story mid-farm keeps everything built in Free Play, including tools a later chapter unlocks, and Chapter 1 may tick off at once if the player already planted.** My recommendation: keep it that way. Finishing Chapter 1 straight away feels like credit for what they did. The alternative is throwing their work away, which is worse.
4. **Building refusals now always point to the nearest spot that fits, not just in the first minutes.** My recommendation: keep it everywhere. It's a pure help.
