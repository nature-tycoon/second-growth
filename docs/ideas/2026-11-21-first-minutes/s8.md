  1. 0–2.6 s black, sound first: creek and wind, then a Steller's jay (no raven recording in the library yet: question 1).
  2. 2.6–12 s eye level in the old forest north of the farm (session 6's border forest), early-morning sun. A black-tailed doe browses (head down), lifts her head at 9.2 s.
  3. 12–22 s she bolts. The cougar breaks low from cover behind her and chases, threading the trunks. The camera rides alongside on her right.
  4. 22–31 s the top of the waterfall at the farm's north edge, looking up the creek. The doe clears the fence (the game's pounce arc) and bounds out past the falls onto the field. The cougar stops at the last trees and stands watching, while the view slowly tightens on it.

### How to re-film

```bash
nice -n 15 node tools/film-intro.mjs
```
That films all six layers into `dist/intro-film/frames/` (about a minute on the M5), assembles, encodes, checks playback and rewrites every file above. Look at `dist/intro-film/samples/game/` (a frame a second) before committing. Then `node tools/intro-film-check.mjs --live`.
- Change a shot: edit its camera or staging in `tools/intro-film.mjs` (`filmLayer`), then `--layers=<name>` films just that layer and reassembles. Timings, fades, time of day and sound are in `tools/intro-film-shots.mjs`; `--assemble` alone remixes the sound without refilming.
- `--no-assemble` films without encoding, to look at frames first.
- Re-film when the look changes a lot, or when `intro-film-check` fails (the start view or start time moved).

## Open questions for Cole

1. **No raven.** The library has no raven recording, and decision 0002 says to ask before downloading. A Steller's jay calls at 1 s instead. *Recommendation:* yes, download a CC0/CC BY common raven from Xeno-canto or Freesound (a few hundred KB). It also gives the forest edge the raven session 5 suggested. I'd swap it into `SOUND` and re-assemble (no refilming).
2. **The first-visit start view isn't the one the code describes.** main.js calls `resetView()` before the renderer has a world, so PNW opens at (44, 38), not the map-size middle (43.2, 37.8) the code intends. The film matches what players actually see (44, 38), and `intro-film-check` holds that. *Recommendation:* leave it. It's a 0.8-tile difference nobody notices. If it is ever fixed, re-film the last two layers (`--layers=overview,start`).
3. **The doe jumps the boundary fence in the film, but in the game deer wait at it** (session 3's opening). Deer clear 2 m fences when chased, so it's real, but a sharp-eyed player might ask why theirs wait. *Recommendation:* keep it. It's the escape, and the opening's "tear out the fence" is about everyday movement, not a chase.
4. **The waterfall reads from above (in the rise), not at the top-of-the-falls shot.** There the eye looks up the creek and the drop is just under the frame. *Recommendation:* fine for now. A version looking back down over the lip would show the fall but lose the forest edge and the cougar.
5. **Sound credits.** Several recordings are CC BY-SA (jay, wren, thrush, meadowlark, robin). The film should credit them where it is shown: itch page, website, and the game's existing audio credits cover the in-game copy. *Recommendation:* one line under the video on itch and the website, "Sounds: NPS, Freesound and Xeno-canto recordists (CC BY-SA), see credits", linking the credits page.
6. **The 1080p WebM (8.8 MB) isn't used by anything yet.** *Recommendation:* keep it until the website plays the film; drop it if the site only ever uses the MP4.

## New ideas noticed

- A real **rewind**: 3–4 frames of the farm at years 20, 10 and 5 (from a seeded time-lapse run) flashed during the dissolve would read as time running back, not a crossfade.
- The **film's restored farm** could be the player's real farm 30 years on: run the time-lapse headless from the start with a standard planting and film that, rather than release-fixtures' restored map.
- The film works as a **trailer** as is. A 9:16 cut of the chase and the rise (the website's `--vertical` approach) would suit Shorts and TikTok.
- The forest shots would gain a lot from **snags, nurse logs and light shafts** in the border forest (session 6's idea). At eye level the border is mostly evenly spaced trunks.
- The cougar's watch at the edge could use the game's **ear and tail idle**. It stands very still.
