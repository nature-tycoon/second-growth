## Done

- **Animals out in the border stand on its land** (f1.md question 5). The drawing read the ground through a clamp to 9 tiles outside the farm, but the border is drawn 14 tiles out (`BORDER`). Past 9 tiles an animal stood at the height of the 9-tile line. Measured on the five land maps, that sank it by up to 2.6 scene units (PNW), 1.5 (Sumatra), 1.35 (Atlanta) and about 0.5 (Serengeti, Amazon). The animal's height, its slope pitch and a kill lying in the border now all read the whole drawn border (`actors-common.js`, `actors-ground.js slopePose`, `actors.js` carcasses; a kill was clamped to the farm itself).
- **A crouching walker folds its legs** (f3.md question 2). The stalking crouch (and a frozen rabbit's half crouch) still lowers the body by a third of a leg. Now each leg also folds at the knee by exactly that much, so the paws stay on the ground: elbows go back, knees forward, paws under the hips. Drawing only. It applies to every walker that crouches (cats, the fox and coyote stalk, a frozen rabbit, monitors, caimans), with no species names. How it works: `headPose` passes the drop in the `fly` slot as `-(2 + drop)`, and the shader's `foldLeg` (`animals/animal-material.js`) bends the leg. A pounce still overrides the crouch. Snakes no longer sink when they stalk, since they already lie along the ground. The rule is written into 0026.
- **Motion sweep now covers both.** It measures `sunk` in the border land too (it read the farm's tiles only), and it no longer excuses a crouch: it measures the paws where the fold draws them (`st.fold`). On the same sweep, the code before this change broke **10 limits**: lion 181, tiger 203, leopard 157, jaguar 116, cheetah 65, ocelot 61, caiman 29, monitor 26, gartersnake 91 and ratsnake 33 sunk frames per 1000, all crouching. After the change it breaks **none** ("All within limits").
- **`farm_opened` is one event again.** Opening a shared farm now sends `shared_farm_opened { map, game_year, from }` (`ui/share.js`). The event list in `js/analytics.js` and decision 0047 say so.
- **The fence warning's `keep` tag is gone.** Session 3 had already removed the tag where it was set, so only the check for it in `ui.holdInFirstMinutes` was left. I removed that check and the fence case in `first-minutes-browser-check`. In a first visit's quiet minutes, a fence-blocked arrival elsewhere now goes to the journal like other warnings. The opening's own fence prompt is a tip with a button, so it isn't affected.

Checks: motion sweep (above), share-file-check, species-name-count and perf-guard-check pass. In the browser, intro-film-motion (all 310 measures), play-analytics and first-minutes (at 812×375 and 1280×800) pass, so the shader compiles and draws. **Not checked by eye:** the folded legs were never looked at in a screenshot.
