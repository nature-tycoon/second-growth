# Session 10/10: Minutes 5–15

## What the leave data said (PostHog, read-only, `game_left` since Nov 12)

571 `game_left` events; 62 left between minute 5 and 15. Of those 62: 46 had lost a species that
session, 51 saw a warning, 28 hit "unsuitable ground", 14 hit "not enough money", 13 had no goal
open, and the median time since their last reward was 96 s (26 s for those leaving at 2–5 min).

- **"Species lost" was mostly migrants leaving for winter.** On PNW the top "lost" species are
  Swainson's thrush (44 sessions), little brown bat (41) and rufous hummingbird (22): all seasonal,
  all announced as "Heading south for the winter". The metric counted them as losses, so the 32 of
  62 "lost a species" in the usage review overstated it. The game itself never told a player about
  a real local loss at all: the species count just dropped.
- **The money refusal was the warning most seen before leaving**: 21 of the sessions that left at
  2–15 minutes saw "Not enough money. Monthly grants will top up your budget…", more than any other.
  Next: invasives spreading (about 28 sessions across wordings), the fence (now the opening), the
  trailhead hint, and "There isn't enough habitat yet" for releases.
- Testers aren't tagged on `game_left` (no `tester` property in the taxonomy), so these numbers
  include Cole and Jacob. Small, but worth knowing.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Should the cougar step stay the head of the tracker even when a nearer goal is closer to
   done?** Recommendation: yes. The film promises the cougar, the step it shows is always the next
   one on the way, and the nearer ordinary goal is one tap away inside it. If players open it rarely
   (`goal_tracker_opened`), show the nearer goal under the head instead.
2. **Is "Deer on the field" worth its own goal, given the fence goal pays for the same click?**
   Recommendation: keep it. It pays once the deer actually walk in, not on the click, and it's the
   first tick on the road to the cougar, so the tracker opens at "1/4" for most first visits.
3. **Natural losses in the journal only: too quiet?** Recommendation: keep it quiet. A vole crash is
   not something to fix, and the "warn" loss lines cover what the player can act on. Revisit if
   players ask where a species went.
4. **Grants for the chain ($600 / $1,200 / $1,800 / $2,400 paid)**: more money on PNW for players who
   get there. Recommendation: keep; it only pays for real milestones, the cougar's lands about year 8
   for a thorough player, and the seeded balance didn't move.

5. **Story mode players get the chain's grants and "Goal complete" toasts too, without the tracker**
   (every catalog goal pays in both modes, as the milestone grants always have). Recommendation:
   keep; they're listed under Milestone grants in the Goals panel. If it feels noisy in chapters,
   pay the chain only in free play.

## New ideas noticed

- **Tag testers on `game_left` too**: the `tester` role is a super property (session 1) but it
  doesn't show in `game_left`'s taxonomy yet; check it's registered before the first event of a session.
- **Most-seen warnings after money**: "Invasives are spreading" (about 28 sessions) and "There isn't
  enough habitat yet" for releases. The invasives one is a fair warning, but in the first year it
  could say which tool pulls them (it says "pull it, burn it, or shade it out").
- **"Unsuitable ground" is still the most common failure** (28 of 62 mid-session leavers). Session 2
  made the refusal name a mix that fits; the brush could also show the green preview before the first
  stroke of a new mix, not only after a refusal.
- **The HUD species count drops every fall** when the migrants leave. A small "(+3 away for winter)"
  next to it would stop the count looking like a loss.
- The owl's moment can land at night (owls hunt at dusk and dark). It reads fine with the moonlight,
  but a dusk owl would look better; the moment could wait for the first hunt in twilight.
