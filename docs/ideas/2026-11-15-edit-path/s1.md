# Session 1: one terrain edit path + roads via Demolish

Branch `claude/ideas-edit-path`. Decision [0048](../../decisions/0048-water-and-elevation-model.md), phase 2.

## Done

- **One edit path.** `js/terrain-edit.js` has `terrainEdit`, which every player height change goes
  through: dig pond, marsh and creek (`digWater`), Raise and Lower ground (`shiftGround`), Fill & grade
  (`gradeTile`), and a building the player puts up (`levelBuilding`). Map generation keeps its own
  passes, and generated maps are byte-for-byte the same on every map (hashed, two seeds each).
- **Every corner has an owner**: a building, a creek or the river, a pond or marsh, or the land. An
  edit moves land corners only, except where water dug up to other water joins it (it may come down to
  that water's bed, never below) and where filling part of a pond raises its shore. The per-tool copies
  of these rules (`carve`'s keep-water, `levelPond`'s skips, `gradeTile`'s holds, `gradeDugCreek`'s
  fixed corners) are gone.
- **Bugs this fixes**: Raise and Lower beside a creek moved the creek's corners (55 cm on pnw,
  Atlanta and the Amazon); a pond dug near a building lifted the building's corner (by its bank); a pond
  dug beside a marsh could pull the marsh's bed below its water; a building put up beside a creek moved
  the creek's corners.
- **Roads are Demolish-only**, like gravel lots: Fill & grade levels a road and leaves it, with one
  hint ("…To take up a road or a gravel lot people laid, use Remove → Demolish."). Tool text updated in
  English, Spanish and Sumatra's own wording.
- **Checks**: new `tools/terrain-edit-check.mjs` (each land tool beside a creek, pond and building on
  every map; a shed beside a creek; no height writes outside the edit path). It fails on the old code.
  `tool-actions-check` gained a road test on every map. Passing: dug-creek, hollis-water, tool-actions,
  fish-water, terrain-relief, water, perf-guard, beaver-pond, beaver-dam, ground-patch, riverbank,
  animal-positioning, plant-performance, species-name-count, tool-actions-browser. Calibrate pnw: score
  61, nothing moved.
- 0048 marked phase 2 done, with what was built and how it differs from the sketch.

## Open questions for Cole

1. **Raise/Lower on a creek tile still reshapes that creek** (its own corners), which can undo the
   grading that lets fish swim a dug creek. Should Raise/Lower refuse creek tiles, the way they refuse
   the river? *Recommended: yes, refuse creek tiles; reshape a creek by filling and re-digging it.
   Ponds and marshes can still be deepened with Lower.*
2. **Ownership is read when an edit runs, not stored**, which is simpler than the plan's "derived
   array". Fine to keep it that way until phase 3 needs channels as their own objects? *Recommended:
   yes.*

## New ideas noticed

- **Fill & grade priced by earth moved** didn't fall out naturally: grading a flat tile costs the same
  as cutting a metre off a slope. `terrainEdit` already knows each corner's old and new height, so it
  could return the earth moved, and the tool could charge by it. A small follow-up.
- **Undo for terrain is a full copy of the height grid** (`undo.js`); `terrainEdit` could record just
  the corners it moved, which phase 3's undo invariant (heights, channels and bodies restored exactly)
  would want anyway.
- The roads hint and the gravel hint share one flag, so a player who has seen one doesn't see the
  other. They say the same thing now, so this seems right; noting it in case it's not.
