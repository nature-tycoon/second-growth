The game's loading screen shows the app icon (`assets/icon-256.png`) and the wordmark (`.ls-o`, `assets/brand-o.svg`, Bricolage Grotesque 700 loaded in the game's `index.html` for just those letters); since 2026-11-14 it is in the website's look: night turning to dawn over the landscape being loaded while its land grows back (pioneers, then trees, then birds or fish), drawn by the inline script after `#loading-screen` from its `LANDS` table (each map's place line, hills, plants and field notes; a new map needs an entry there, its colours under `.loading-screen[data-map]` in `styles.css`, and Spanish lines for any new note a Spanish-played map shows); `main.js` calls `window.sgLoadingLand(startMap)` once it knows the map. Everything that moves is a CSS transform or opacity so it keeps moving while the main thread builds the scene (the bar is `scaleX`, not width); editing that inline script changes its CSP hash (`node tools/website-security-check.mjs` says so). the game's tab icon is `assets/favicon.svg` (a copy of `website/favicon.svg`; keep them the same). The old badge `assets/logo.svg` is still the top bar's and the postcard's emblem. A rounded card with a dark caption over its picture clips with `clip-path: inset(0 round …)`, not only overflow + border-radius, or the picture's edge shows as a light line round the caption's corners (`.ecology-gallery figure`). Scroll-in motion is CSS only (`animation-timeline: view()`), so browsers without it simply show everything. The game itself still uses Fraunces and Nunito. Recapture stills with `tools/capture-site-content.mjs` (then `--maps=<all seven> --moments`) and `tools/capture-header-review.mjs`, under `nice -n 15`; look at each image before keeping it. Contact and follow links: info@secondgrowth.earth (Porkbun forward to Cole's Gmail) and the itch.io page. The keystone reel is six staged studies (lions and vultures in the Serengeti, a blacktip on the reef, a hawk in Atlanta, the elk rut, the hatchlings), filmed at 1600×900 so a full scroll-through stays about 15 MB; Cole cut the tiger, harpy and anaconda clips; plan a new one with `node tools/website-clip-lab.mjs <clip>` (what each animal does every half second of the clip; `SEED=n` tries another run seed, which is how the hawk clip was chosen: in many runs its stoop misses), and give it `g.selectedAgent` (the tracking lens) and a `follow` shot when it flies or climbs, or the crowns hide it. Climbing primates read badly from above and were dropped. Video loops: `nice -n 15 node tools/capture-site-video.mjs` regenerates every clip in `tools/website-video-clips.mjs` (`--clips=a,b` for some, `--vertical` for 9:16 versions) into `website/review-media/video/` with `video-provenance.json`, in under a minute on the M5. It films the production renderer frame by frame at 30 fps on the GPU and encodes in headless Chrome with WebCodecs (H.264 MP4, VP9 WebM; no ffmpeg), refilming at a lower bitrate when a file passes its cap (4.6 MB; the full-screen hero and descent are filmed at 2560×1440 with about three times the bitrate under a 9.4 MB cap, `scale`/`bitrate`/`budget` in the clip list, because at 2.9 Mbit/s the encoders smeared grass and animals to half their rendered detail); look at its sample frames in `dist/website-video-frames/` and the clips at `tools/website-video-review.html` (the dev server now answers byte ranges, which Safari needs for video). Gotchas: a crossfade loop needs a `closed` camera path, or the dissolve shows two views of the land at once, and a moment whose animals walk off loops with a cut (`loop: 'none'`); the calendar is held while filming so no daily plant redraw lands mid-shot. `website-check` validates the video hashes and the 5 MB cap (10 MB for the hero and descent) and warns when any site media was captured more than 30 days or 80 game commits before the current game. The landing page plays that footage: the hero flyover, the scroll-played descent (mouse and wide screen: the footage pinned while the title and four cards scroll up over it, 150vh of scrolling; elsewhere a plain loop) and the keystone reel, all started by `site.js` after load and never with reduced motion or Save-Data. Each clip sits over a lazy picture of its first frame, because a `poster` attribute downloads before the hero. Seeking needs byte ranges: a `tools/serve.py` started before range support was added (2026-11-12) can't seek, so restart an old one; the descent then falls back to playing on a loop rather than freezing on its first frame. A hidden preview pane runs no animation frames or observers, so take a screenshot to make the page paint before reading its state. Field guide (`/guide/`, a page per species): `tools/build-website.mjs` generates it from the map catalogs (`tools/field-guide-data.mjs`, `tools/build-field-guide.mjs`), so a new species gets a page on the next deploy, but it needs a portrait first: `nice -n 15 node tools/capture-field-guide.mjs --only=<slug>` (the game's field-guide renderer at 8×, `setPortraitScale`, saved as a 1200×900 portrait and a 480×360 card, butterfly wings on a 14× finer grid via `setWingDetail`; whole set a few minutes), or `website-check` fails. Calls come from `MAP_RECORDINGS` birds or a recording named for the species. Each landscape's guide colour and portrait stage are `[data-land="<map id>"]` in `website/guide.css` (a new map needs one, and a `review-media/landscape-<id>.jpg` still for its banner); `guide.js` runs the index filters and the call player, and every page works without it.

Search (2026-11-19, plan and data in [seo-plan-2026-11.md](seo-plan-2026-11.md)): guide pages lead with the species, not the game (title "Name (Latin): field guide | Second Growth", description the real fact first, `og:type` article) and carry a food-web paragraph built from the game's data (`prey`/`challengePrey` give "It hunts" and the reverse "It is hunted by", linked page to page; `frugivore`, `browseRate`, `damBuilder`; plants' bloom and fruit months, `mast`, `graze`/`browse`, leaves, `nfix`). Only write sentences the sim makes true. Help pages (`/help/<topic>/`, `tools/build-help.mjs`, the guide's `page()` layout) answer what players search for; each cites the code its claims come from, so change the page when that code changes. The sitemap's `<lastmod>` is the last commit touching each page's sources (`sitemapDates` in `build-website.mjs`); a shallow clone gives none, so `pages.yml` checks out full history without blobs. After each deploy `pages.yml` runs `tools/indexnow.mjs` against the sitemap that was live before, sending new and re-dated pages to Bing and the other IndexNow engines; the key is `website/<key>.txt` (public by design), and the step never fails a deploy. The game's `index.html` (the site's `/play/`) has its own title, canonical and share tags; `website-check` holds them.

Intro film (2026-11-21, first-minutes session 8; how-to in [docs/ideas/2026-11-21-first-minutes/s8.md](ideas/2026-11-21-first-minutes/s8.md)): a 31.8 s first-visit film of the PNW farm (re-staged in the film rework, f3.md), staged and filmed from the real game by `nice -n 15 node tools/film-intro.mjs` (shots, animals, cameras and trims in `tools/intro-film-plan.mjs`; layers and sound in `tools/intro-film-shots.mjs`, derived from it; staging and assembly in `tools/intro-film.mjs`; a few minutes in all). The game's 1280×720 copy is `assets/intro/intro-film.mp4` (+ `.webm`), 5 MB cap; its last frame (`intro-film-last.jpg`) and camera (`intro-film.json`) are the live first view, which `tools/intro-film-check.mjs` holds (headless; `--live` compares against the real game). The 1920×1080 copy for the website and itch.io is `website/review-media/video/intro-film-1080.mp4` (+ `.webm`), 10 MB cap, its provenance in `intro-film.json` (website-check reads it there). Its MP4 is also the game's `assets/intro/intro-film-1080.mp4` (2026-11-22, the same bytes, written by `film-intro.mjs` as the site output's `copies`): `intro.js` `filmSize` plays it first, before the 720p, where the film is drawn at least 600 CSS and 900 device pixels tall and the connection is 4G or unknown without Save-Data, so laptops and desktops but not phones; a 1080p that fails falls through to the 720p sources, and `START_GRACE_MS` still lets the game start without it. No 1080p WebM in the game (2% smaller, and every browser that picks it plays H.264). It adds 7.5 MB to the itch.io package, not to any player's download (itch.io serves HTML5 files one by one; a player fetches only the copy picked). `intro_started` reports `size` (chosen) and `played`. Sound is muxed in (AAC in MP4, Opus in WebM, `tools/video-mux.mjs`); the recordings it uses are credited in `intro-film.json`, several CC BY-SA. Gotcha: the check browser's server answers no byte ranges, so a video loaded from it cannot seek; load it from a blob URL to seek. The game plays it on a first visit to PNW (`js/ui/intro.js`, session 9): over the loading screen, once per device (`second-growth-intro-seen`), skippable, then "Watch intro" in the start menu and the menu. It is off under automation (`navigator.webdriver`, every headless check and capture tool) unless the address has `?intro=1`; `?intro=0` turns it off for a player. `tools/intro-browser-check.mjs` covers it. Film rework (f1.md): `node tools/intro-film-motion.mjs` checks the staging headless without rendering (subject in sight, real speeds, hooves on the ground, the chase, camera smoothness, plants and sky over the view; in the browser suite as `intro-film-motion-browser-check.mjs`, before each itch.io upload); `film-intro.mjs --rough` makes a 360p 10 fps cut in seconds; review cuts and leave timed notes at `http://localhost:8347/tools/film-review.html` (contact sheets in `dist/intro-film/contact/`, not `assets/`, which ships).
