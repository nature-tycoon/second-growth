**Play the newest version, see every species in the field guide, and get the monthly update at [secondgrowth.earth](https://secondgrowth.earth).**

Second Growth is a free game about bringing damaged land back to life. Take a worn-out farm, a cleared rainforest or a bleached reef, plant natives, reopen creeks and migration routes, and see which of about 200 animal species come back. Then drop to eye level and walk among them.

**Seven landscapes, each based on a real place**
- Hollis Farm, Pacific Northwest: an old dairy farm with a blocked salmon creek
- Fazenda Esperança, Brazilian Amazon: a cattle ranch cut from the rainforest
- Enkare Conservancy, Serengeti: an overgrazed range fenced across a migration route
- Magnolia Ridge, Georgia: a suburb built over oak woodland
- Finca El Guanacaste, Nicaragua: a cattle farm running down to Pacific mangroves (also in Spanish)
- Kalinda Reef, Great Barrier Reef: a reef damaged by heatwaves
- Kebun Tualang, North Sumatra: cleared forest and drained peat swamp

**What happens on its own**
- 215 native plants, each with its own moisture, light and soil needs. Pioneers rebuild the soil, slower trees move in under the canopy, dead trees become snags, logs and soil.
- 194 animal species that move in, raise young and leave based on what's really there. Predators stalk and chase, prey learns to avoid kill sites, scavengers clean up. You never buy animals.
- Keystone moments: salmon running up the creek, beavers building their first dam, the wildebeest crossing, the elk rut, sea turtle hatchlings running for the sea.
- Seasons, migration, wildfires and floods. Controlled burns are cheap, but in dry weather they can get away from you.
- Watch mode and eye level: let the camera find the wildlife, or walk among it.

**Also**
- A story campaign on several maps, or free play with every tool from the start.
- Save your farm as a file to keep or send to a friend (share links come once the new server is live; add "a link anyone can open" then).
- Plays on phones (turn sideways).
